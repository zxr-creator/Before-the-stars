## Cross-system consequences

The state remains deep where choices interact: one supply of skilled hours supports education, production, research, care and military service; knowledge must become reproducible engineering and actual capacity; contracts move rights and resources over time; local institutions govern participation; occupation requires restored services. Simplification removes object and matching overhead rather than these constraints.

No fixed budget ratio is declared optimal. More education can remove a long-lead bottleneck or leave unaffordable unused skills. Research concentration can be right when complements exist. Military readiness can protect a future that a peaceful spreadsheet ignores. Adaptive policies can also overbuild, misread demand or prepare for the wrong threat.

The steam/diesel/electrical paper case remains an instructive conditional comparison. With reliable hydro and copper, E completes industrial acceptance at M48, S at M60 and D at M72. The common hydro shortfall changes fixed E to M84; a paid lease changes it to M54, thirty months faster than waiting at an additional net ending-cash cost of424.50 c. This preserves a large legitimate decision benefit without a style bonus. These are local industrial dates, not FTL win times or measured AI performance.

## Endgame compatibility case

The new [competitive scenario](../design/33-competition-and-native-ai-v1.0.md) is an explicitly conditional postindustrial checkpoint. Rivals trade while retaining independent victory interests, attempt alternatives/coalitions and do not voluntarily award the leader victory by default. The selected branch assumes actual paid military/political integration, not an automatically successful attack.

Its windows reconcile: governance M121–216 contains96 months; military capability M193–216 contains24; independent components complete M193/M205 and become available M194/M206; trials use an existing qualified P0 atM193 and C1 atM205, twelve months apart; three-node work occurs M194–199; replacement/relief atM207. The case's H360 produces conditional reward1.40, distinct from the standard H2880. Missing battle/economic schedules remain declared assumptions. The historical cooperative M336 case is retained without being relabeled as this result.

Forfeiture counterexample: an active leader at G95/F23 withdraws. Its caretaker may preserve service into the next month, but the original seat stays−1 and cannot terminate active rivals' match as winner. No new controller identity inherits eligibility. If every original seat is forfeited/eliminated, the referee ends without a winner.

Voting counterexample: a member that already delegated an ordinary policy to a charter's majority process refuses mutual chat. A valid fixed ballot can still reach the institutional registry; the old electorate, threshold and notice rules govern. This does not allow arbitrary new sovereignty/asset promises through an exempt ballot. Renaming delegates does not multiply votes, and an amendment cannot lower its own passing threshold.

## What the review does not establish

- No proof that the standard 240-round primitive-to-FTL campaign is economically feasible yet; that is a P4 gate.
- No proof that a dominant strategy is absent, a stronger native difficulty always wins, or single-winner diplomacy avoids every deadlock.
- No historical claim that real civilizations have innate technological/cultural rankings. Registration loss is not automatically deaths.
- No runtime conservation, protocol, isolation, multiplayer or performance test. Source-document checks cannot replace them.
- No generated final art assets, renderer or playable Mac/web build. The release is the complete current design baseline and review package.

## Validation required before implementation and release

Freeze all referenced content units, upstream recipes, discovery/acceptance rules, demographic response tables, AI work budgets, authority predicates and observation whitelists. Then build conservation, split/merge, idempotency, hidden-state noninterference, thread-count replay and adversarial protocol fixtures before full matches.

Use matched seeds/starts/opponents to compare department priorities, staged investment, specialization, autarky, industrial transitions, licensing, joint research, denial and redundant supply. Retain failed and unresolved games and confidence intervals. Distinguish real economic decisions from resource/rights/time duplication. A successful surprising strategy is not a defect simply because it wins decisively.
