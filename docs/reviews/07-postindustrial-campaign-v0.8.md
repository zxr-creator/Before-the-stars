# 0.8 Historical Cooperative Case: Four Civilizations from Industry to Complete Victory

**Archived design case, not a current mode.** This v0.8 example used the former cooperative setting in which four original seats could share victory through actual common governance and joint engineering. v1.0 removes shared victory: all official games are competitive with at most one winner. The original quantities, M336 outcome, and historical cooperative reward below remain unchanged to preserve the audit. They do not prove that competing opponents surrender victory. The counterfactual section examines that conflict; [chapter 33](../design/33-competition-and-native-ai-v1.0.md) supplies a separate new competitive conditional case. The physical endpoint remains [G96 global integration, F24 military-wide FTL capability, and two trials](../design/15-victory-and-ftl.md), never an industrial score.

This is a design case with resources, hours, stage accounts, and dates, not an executed simulation. M0 is a postindustrial checkpoint; M336 is not 336 months from primitive society. Population, local consumption, and ordinary treasury activity are explicit controlled background conditions rather than endogenous replay. New joint engineering, training, licenses, assets, and duties have separate accounts. Use [product interfaces](../design/21-production-chains-v0.7.md), [work packages](../design/25-ai-cyber-bio-industries-v0.8.md), and [course semantics](../design/19-education-and-transmission-v0.6.md). FTL quantities are an independently sealed scenario specification; do not mix other examples' quotes/yields. Complete technology/culture graphs are fixed; local knowledge, adoption, practices, and investment vary without redrawing nodes or scientific prerequisites.

The current candidate standard is twelve simulation months per round and H2880/240 rounds. This archived checkpoint uses its own explicitly stated H480 accounting and is not directly comparable to standard ranked results.

## 1. Four actors in one world

There are 24 inhabited nodes and 1.2 million residents. Each civilization initially governs six nodes/300,000 people. All have basic supply, operating industry, and scientific method. They are not single-profession species; investment can remedy weaknesses. Advantages derive from facilities, knowledge, contracts, and personnel.

Clearbay's model is not a material-truth oracle, and Greenreach's expertise reveals no untested ecosystem success probability.

## 6. Why licensing, personnel, and diplomacy can matter greatly

**A license is not a percentage bonus.** Riverland buys specified documents, mentors, and local acceptance. Both alternatives start M1 and include M1–24 preparation/waiting; identical school/equipment costs remain payable in both. Independent same-scope development costs 12,000 including verification/correction, finishes M72, usable M73. Licensing costs 6,000 plus buyer retest 300. After M55 qualification, integration/stable records continue to M60, usable M61: **5,700 c saved and twelve months earlier**. Seller cure 500 is in seller investment and is not charged twice to buyer.

These are finite quotes/scope, not universal trade discounts. Different local preparation costs require further adjustment. Independent development may grant broader rights or become necessary when the seller refuses.

**Earlier training matters, but course delay is not automatically victory delay.** Existing E016/E017 and research foundations make E023 available M109, allowing E024 after M192 prototype. If missing 36-month role training starts only M193, then E024 earliest M229–276, qualifications M277. The same interfaces/preparation move from M241–288 to M277–324, procedures usable M325. However, original M289–312 has scheduling slack, and G96 does not finish until M336. Therefore a 36-month course delay does **not** prove a 36-month victory delay. Reschedule manufacture, trials, and F24 before claiming a new completion time.

The former inference M336 + 36 = M372 is removed. The demonstrated benefit is qualification 36 months earlier and earlier interface preparation. How much reaches victory depends on longest paths/slack. Players should identify which of facilities, people, and politics actually controls completion rather than always maximize the costliest course.

**Cooperation retains bargaining.** Farcape can refuse cheap precision access; Riverland can demand sustained orders before expansion; Greenreach can seek failure/resupply sharing; Clearbay can retain training-data rights while selling narrow models. Actual complementary capability is what matters. Quotes, exit duties, territorial rights, and renewal can save or cost years.

**Integration may need to start before final technology.** Governance M241 gives G96 at M336. With unchanged technology but actual integration only M289, G96 earliest M384, while FTL must remain viable. Signing a global alliance at M335 cannot purchase 95 past months. Large early-integration gains do not justify secret penalties.

**AI and biology are not mandatory victory taxes.** The case uses narrow AI, ordinary biological manufacture, and water recovery—not AI supreme rule, high-bandwidth interfaces, reliable regeneration, or full ecology. Human teams can substitute more labor; resupplied spaceflight can meet tasks. Further A3, cyber assistance, or B3 investment depends on observable constraints, duration, and cost.

## 10. Exploit and rule risks exposed

- **Model copying versus compute duplication:** lawful copies distribute knowledge; every execution still uses device-hours, power, memory, and rights. Copies create neither people nor research speed.
- **One dataset counted as four independent results:** sharing is legitimate; provenance deduplicates trial identity/correlation. Independent validation needs real processes.
- **Double-counted fund/treasury:** 280,000 is deducted from government balances. Fund payments occur once; refunds/dividends cannot recreate spent 242,000.
- **Failure erases knowledge/refunds all wages:** documents, services, and result guarantees settle separately. Accepted learning, teaching, and consumed failed research remain.
- **Recovery treated as net new supply:** water/nutrients derive from actual waste with losses/resupply. Wastewater and recovered water cannot both be drinkable stock.
- **Prototype, completion, and availability conflated:** C1/C2 are usable next month. Actual manufacture/payload work satisfies tests, not titles or data files.
- **Last signature, AI takeover, or temporary borrowing invents historical integration:** audit actual authority, services, and expiry monthly. Renaming/upgrading/resigning creates no past months.
- **Cooperative arithmetic conceals competitive incentives:** preserve this archived case's historical scoring and explicitly reject it as current competitive proof. Authorial hopes are not opponents' strategies.

These corrections preserve large legitimate gains from first moves, rights, personnel, and diplomacy. The next step is complete local recipes, batch transport, regional supply, and bounded actor behavior, followed by multiworld adversarial tests. This case shows that its stated resource ceilings, cash stages, courses, and windows can be interpreted consistently. It does not prove the endogenous economy reaches this path or that no globally dominant strategy exists.
