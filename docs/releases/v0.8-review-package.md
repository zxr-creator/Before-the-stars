## Clarifications made at this stage

1. Complete nodes, AND/OR dependencies, alternatives, discovery and adoption rules stay identical within a content version. Seeds change world/social starts and actual-event draws. Dynamic experience comes from knowledge, investment, geography, culture, religion and adoption. Core documents and interfaces were synchronized.
2. AI/cyber/bio are accepted by specific task, without national universal multipliers. Human work, ordinary software, limited networks and resupplied space operations remain alternatives. AI rule and complete ecological closure are not mandatory victory prerequisites.
3. Legitimate nonexclusive knowledge sharing can produce large gains. Equipment, compute, teacher hours, batches and exclusive rights cannot be copied with files. Independent verification uses available evidence, not secret truth supplied as an anti-exploit oracle.
4. Earlier graduation does not necessarily mean earlier victory: check slack in equipment/deployment. A missing 36-month course cannot automatically be called a 36-month victory delay. Licensing comparisons align preparation, cure, start month and scope.
5. The historical release distinguished shared and sole-winner modes. Mutual trade does not imply willingness to surrender winning authority. v1.0 now removes shared winners while retaining legal cooperation and actual changes of government.

## Numerical results and limits

In the specified P-A1 task, 120 monthly person-hours became 40 operating plus 20 review hours and 100 CB. Construction cost 2,000 c; net monthly savings of 80 c required 25 accepted operating months to repay it. At one-tenth demand, that AI configuration cost 70 c/month versus 24 c manually. Scale, supervision and actual demand matter; this does not establish one cost curve for all AI.

The four-civilization joint project spent 242,000 c and retained 38,000 c. Governments, fund and other actors still totaled 2,000,000 c. Joint materials remained below stated supply bounds; education counted teachers, learners and practice separately. This was stage-level accounting, not a full monthly schedule of every job, facility, household and shipment.

Under the stated performance/supply assumptions, governance M241–336 lasted 96 months and FTL capability M313–336 lasted 24. Trials M313/M325 were twelve months apart; components became usable the month after completion. Historical cooperative completion was M336 on a postindustrial local clock. M0 was already industrial, not a claim of primitive-to-FTL development in 28 years. Ordinary welfare and some supply were controlled boundaries, not endogenous simulation. This result is not current competitive completion evidence.

Checks covered document links, content IDs/dependencies, local task arithmetic, stage cash/materials, courses, final windows and paper exploit cases. They were not runtime tests, exclusion of optimal strategies or proof of no vulnerabilities. Remaining gates include complete parameters, task/error response, actual upstreams, monthly capability scheduling, population/economy and adversarial opponents.
