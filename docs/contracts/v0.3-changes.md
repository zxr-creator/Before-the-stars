# v0.3 written contract and migration checklist

Historical design. See [v0.4 changes](v0.4-changes.md) and the [v1.0 addendum](v1.0-design-addendum.md) for subsequent authority. Statements that work remained incomplete describe the original stage; v0.4 later supplied core war and contract transitions.

Status: a design proposal, without generated schema, DTOs or SDK. Historical v0.1/v0.2 JSON and fixtures do not validate the current protocol. The [agent protocol](../technical/02-agent-protocol.md) and gameplay specifications define current semantics.

## Retained and replaced behavior

Retain seat authentication, observation snapshots, idempotency, phase sealing, decimal quantity strings, basis points and opaque handles. The historical target envelope version was `0.3`; changing an old payload's version string does not migrate it. Until generated migration exists, there is no releasable current machine contract.

| Area | v0.3 requirement | Migration constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CulturalProfileView | `ref, name, population_report_refs, observed_practice_refs, descriptions, report_refs`; each description has `source_ref, observed_month, scope_ref` | Remove internal axes and numerical estimates; do not publish eight axes instead of six. Private lineage updates do not automatically alter public handles |
| Social effects and conditions | Known materials, hours, facilities, costs and rights may be listed; social explanations belong to attributed Reports | No causal formulas in `known_effects`; no hidden acceptance thresholds or their Boolean truth in `known_requirements` |
| Settlement/education views | EntityView whitelist includes node function, verifiable capacity, reachable links, enrollment/graduation and coverage months | No complete private skill distributions, cultural memories or response weights; national totals do not replace local data |
| Primitive stage | Zero cash is allowed; actual in-kind inputs, labor, organizing rights and template choices still validate | Zero cash is not zero cost; no monetary-market handle before that institution exists |
| market_order | Add `mode: once / standing`; share product, location, side and price limit | Once uses `quantity`; standing uses fields below, never both quantity semantics |
| Standing purchases | Require `monthly_quantity_cap, target_inventory, monthly_cash_cap, end_month`; initially buying only | Nonnegative quantity strings; safe-integer month; generate from own known inventory. Unfilled monthly quantity expires rather than accumulating indefinitely |
| manage_market_order | `policy_ref, operation: amend / cancel`; amend supplies a complete replacement configuration | Plan only; cancel has no new configuration; no duplicate same-policy override in one batch; completed trades do not roll back |
| propose_treaty | Replace `expires_round` with `registry_ref, expires_month`; optional actual `custody_ref` | Registry is a real place/organization, not automatic custody or judicial power. Necessary parties must be known and reachable |
| sign_treaty | Retain proposal version and `escrow_cash_cap`; add `escrow_asset_refs` for authorized goods/collateral | Reserve an asset once; cash-free signatures may impose goods obligations; unsigned parties' assets cannot be locked |
| goods_delivery | Choose cash payment or `counter_delivery_ref`; optional `min_lot_quantity, acceptance_ref` | Barter references the reciprocal delivery in the same version. No implicit triangular netting; escrow exchange requires both deliveries |
| technology_license | Milestones each have unique ID, delivery type, known acceptance plan, inputs, deadline and fee | Accept rights, records, training and engineering tests separately. A rights-only license is explicit and does not guarantee mass production |
| joint_research | Each party's cash/goods and `compute_commitments`; milestone contribution conditions and result rights | Compute specifies facility, month, quantity and network; no contribution counted twice. Voluntary gifts are explicit |
| access | `start_month, end_month, monthly_fee` plus capacity share | Monthly fee is a future obligation, not free full-term access or default full upfront payment. Outages enter interruption/breach states |
| loan | Optional `collateral_refs` to actual escrowed cash/goods; retain principal and simple interest | No unpurchased, already pledged or third-party collateral. Enforcement cannot exceed actual control |
| charter | Optional `amends_charter_ref`; founding requires all necessary consent; amendment follows existing charter powers | An amendment cannot lower the threshold for passing itself. A sole government cannot fabricate federal members |
| Budget and receipts | Separate available, monthly reserved, escrowed, spent and future payable | Do not freeze empty departmental ceilings twice. Sealed does not mean remote registration or engineering success |

The initial standing-sale omission was later superseded by [v0.5](v0.5-design-addendum.md). At the v0.3 stage, selling used one-time orders, actual private sales and delivery contracts. Implementers could not silently reinterpret `target_inventory`; extending standing sales required reserve stock, floor price, budget/generation order and versioning.

Acceptance plans and contribution conditions use known templates and bounded structures, not uploaded scripts, natural-language Boolean expressions or secret referee paths. Contracts state verifiability, certification rights and dispute procedures. A representable schema field does not create an institution's missing enforcement capability.

## Execution-state design

| Object | Distinct states | Forbidden shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | Draft, sent/in transit, registered awaiting signatures, withdrawn, expired, formed | Treating an in-transit signature as arrived; retroactive withdrawal after formation |
| Custody | Reserved, actually received, releasable, service interrupted, disposed under known authority, closed | Automatic refund on lost contact; repeatedly pledging one asset |
| Delivery/acceptance | Unsent, in transit, partially received, awaiting test, accepted, paid, disputed, breached, returning | Learning before delivery; refunds erasing transport or acquired knowledge |
| Education/training | Queued, actively training, interrupted, awaiting validation, qualified, maintaining/recovering | Full skill on enrollment; renaming resetting expenditure or elapsed time |

This was a list of required states, not a complete transition table. Triggers, executors, inputs/outputs, timeouts, irreversible effects and visible events—especially custody failures and disputes—needed D0 closure. It was not an implemented secure contract engine.

## Migration and acceptance order

1. Complete transition tables and public-field whitelists, then generate schema, Rust DTOs, TypeScript types and new fixtures.
2. Test old examples only against their old version. Reject old axes, expired date semantics and one-shot license payments mislabeled as v0.3. Automatic migration cannot assume counterpart consent to changed terms.
3. Cover primitive cash-free projects, urban/rural services, standing procurement, milestone licenses, barter, remote signatures, interrupted contributions, collateral and sole-government completion.
4. Test noninterference for invisible culture across observations, queries, help, requirements, errors, history, cursors and caches. Preserve normal explanation and investigation of genuinely visible behavior.
5. Check money, goods, people, labor, rights and responsibilities for duplication. Contract correctness and information authorization are independent; schema validity is not semantic correctness.
