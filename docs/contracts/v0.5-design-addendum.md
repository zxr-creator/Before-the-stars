# v0.5 engine-design requirements for the interface

This is an unimplemented historical written specification. It builds on [v0.4](v0.4-changes.md); v0.1/v0.2 schemas, examples and earlier Rust sketches were not migrated. Freeze the actual current protocol in P0 when fields and generated types agree. Do not claim an existing SDK supports this table. Current overrides are in the [v1.0 addendum](v1.0-design-addendum.md).

This stage retained 18 actions and eight contract types. Operations, labor and known institutions use `market_order`, `manage_market_order`, `set_production`, `set_policy`, projects and contracts; do not expose arbitrary internal variable assignment.

| Capability | Existing entry point and required definition | Observation/feedback | Forbidden leakage or authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standing sales | Standing `market_order` supports buy and sell; selling specifies actual available stock, reserve stock, price limit and expiry | Monthly generation, sold/expired quantity and reserve reasons | No automatic sale of unproduced goods or rights over unauthorized facilities |
| Facility operations | `set_production/set_policy` selects known authorized templates with production, procurement/sales, hiring and maintenance scope | Effective policy and recent execution | Private/public actors use their own known prices and supply, never an omniscient optimizer |
| Jobs and training | Templates define task qualifications, posts, wages/in-kind support, teacher/student time and switching constraints | Authorized demand, actual assignment and unmet quantity | No forced arbitrary private employment, religious commitment or cultural acceptance |
| Wage advances | Explicit payment under existing labor rules, tied to one obligation, advance, month, settlement priority, departmental spending, net settlement and nonperformance | Each employer/household's authorized payments and debts | No bypass of payable limits; future wages are not current cash, credit is not automatic, no double payment |
| Inventory and delivery | Separate accepted usable, reserved, in transit, pending acceptance and newly produced awaiting storage | Quantities, due month and availability under ownership/custody rights | Transit is not local spot inventory; query success cannot reveal foreign hidden stock |
| Monthly services | Known location/month capacity, service specification and inputs | Contracted capacity, actual supply/use and measured failure | No private global dependency graph; known equipment inputs may be public |
| Research work | Real task units, known inputs, measurements and valid evidence in projects/milestones | Team hours, samples, trials, waiting and visible acceptance | No total future-node count, hidden AND/OR requirements or unknown suitability truth |
| Culture/practices | Trials, people and projects through existing policy/project actions | Actual participation and source/coverage-qualified surveys/descriptions | No axes, transmission anchors, experience signals, targets, lambda, direction vectors or true preferences |
| Operating feedback | Metrics/series queries read authorized records | Money, hours, stock change, downtime and waiting with unit/time/source/coverage | A queryable panel is not a free survey; financial forecasts are not future truth |
| Time and versioning | Fixed scenario calendar, phase and legal windows; separate rules/content/generator/protocol versions | When an order takes effect, expires and next becomes observable | No private-event-triggered decision window or future objective encoded in version names |

Preflight checks only known syntax, permissions and verifiable own conditions. Hidden conditions create observable consequences after real execution. Candidate counts, error timing, forecast differences and standing-order eligibility cannot reveal foreign assets, cultural responses or unknown technologies.

Batch, pagination, MCP, CLI and HTTP share one projection. Human panels display those interfaces without extra backend fields; agents obtain equivalent structured data without reading statistics from pixels. Panels and events never reference private primary keys from the internal catalog.

P0 fixtures must cover standing buy/sell policies, public/private symmetry, advances and net wages, arriving goods pending acceptance, service shortfall, cultural secrecy and hidden-state noninterference. These are future acceptance requirements; the original revision checked document links rather than running protocol implementations.
