What happened on the way (each a 32-seed batch):
- b1: the merged graph with the native AI's expected-value research (section 3.2) and the
  design's investigation lines 2 + isqrt(staff / 50,000): printing AD 2100 (late by 700),
  the median seat at 9 M in 2000, the leader at 41% of technologies by 2300. A medieval
  seat has some 30,000 research staff, i.e. two lines.
- b2: lines 4 + isqrt(staff / 10,000) (LINES_BASE, LINES_STAFF in sim::knowledge): no
  change. In these eras every investigable card is started anyway; knowledge is bound by
  evidence, not by lines or by the AI's choice (b3 and b4, with the channel-step and
  economy shares of the research budget, gave the same populations to the decimal).
- The slowdown is the critical recording node T030 understood 100-300 years later than in
  slice 6r in the same worlds, and printing after it; the cause was not isolated (a build
  without families gave the same result in a probe match, so the locks are not it). The
  medieval thresholds of slice 6r sat at the edge of the stagnation basin found there.
- b5 (recorded below): late antiquity 20 M, medieval 14 M, high medieval 10 M (the slice-3
  values) and renaissance 13 M (was 16 M).
