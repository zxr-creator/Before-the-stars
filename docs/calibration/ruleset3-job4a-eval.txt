Steps: the first batch had no station at all. Launch stages standing down at the cap failed
"a working launch complex", so no seat could investigate spaceflight life support while it had
nothing to spend boost on: a stage standing down now counts as working. Station posts competed
with the works for technicians and went unstaffed: station crews are now reserved before the
works are staffed. A founding shipment of a year's supplies (4,200 t) did not fit two chemical
berths (4,000 t): six months' supplies. Lifts (crew supplies) are paid before larger shipments.

Findings for job 4b:
- No seat enters the Space era by 2500 (all era 6 at the end), so the policy's extra launch
  levels never trigger; boost caps stay at one launch complex level. Launch complexes run little
  (short of refined fuel and electricity): about 0.4 Mt of boost was made in all matches.
- Stations stop at a full K1 core (four modules) where the ground orbital station that the K2
  core needs is not built; no shipyard module was placed, so check 13 is not yet met by AI seats.
- Outposts are founded but starve: no resupply reaches the zone store (Earth volatiles are scarce
  without water and air plants, ice works need T159), served share 0, residents die at three
  times the rate and births stop; off-world population peaks near 1,300. An unserved own site
  fails G96's food and water conditions for its owner.
