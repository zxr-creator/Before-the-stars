Steps: raids at design 5.4's 800 bp a month with no rest took about a fifth of a frontier
seat's tools a year and halved mid-game population: a band now rests 24 months after a raid and
a raid adds 300 bp of unrest. A clearing rule that reached camps three links away emptied the
world by 3000 BC; one that waited for blocked expansion left all eight camps to 2000 (admin
capacity, not land, stops expansion mid-game). Kept: clear camps next to the land or blocking
the next settlement when expansion is blocked, every adjacent camp from the powder age, and after
AD 1700 an idle band drifts 10% a year into the nearest governed region. Pairwise market clearing
replaced a multilateral solvency loop: with no credit at the start every seat must buy exactly
what it sells, and the multilateral fixed point collapsed to nothing.
