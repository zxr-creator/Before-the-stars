Against the targets
- Population (design table 36 §3.1, +-35%): the median seat is within it at 1000 BC, AD 1,
  1000, 1500 and 1700 (1.11, 0.71, 0.81, 0.75, 0.65); 3000 BC 1.99x; 1800 0.56; 1900-2200
  0.27-0.37; world peak about 0.96 B against 1.9-3.75 B. The slice-6r batch had 1500-1900
  in band and 2000-2200 at 0.3-0.5; the larger graphs cost the 1800-1950 rows (the leaders
  still boom: upper quartile 55 M in 1800, 112 M in 1900).
- Era windows (table 36 §2.7, median seat): every milestone from storage (T007) to
  electricity (T100) in window or within 20 years except T024 masonry (1,285 years early,
  as in every slice), T030 recording media (270 early) and T036 propellants (115 early);
  the scientific method and programmable computers 20 years late; orbital transport T142
  120 years late; fission and the jump tier reached by a minority (47, 4 and 3 of 128 seats).
- Technology completion at AD 2300 (doc 39 table 1.3): leading seat median 66% (target
  70-85%), median seat 59% (45-65%), highest ever 268/300 (nobody completes the graph).
- Practice completion at AD 2300 (table 2.4): leading seat median 56% (50-65%), median seat
  55% (35-50%, missed), highest ever 101/174 = 58% (never above 75%). Nearly every seat
  reaches the same ceiling of about 96-101 practices by 2300 (one side of each of the 17
  exclusive groups, no tradition institutions, the off-world three behind T170, late hard
  requirements): raising the late costs 1.5-2x (c4) and tightening capacity moved nothing,
  so the median target needs either dearer or more load-bound late practices than the
  design's table, or a native AI that holds fewer; not done.

-------- the Expert test policy skeleton: --ai expert,hard,medium,medium, eval-0..31 --------
No match has a winner (as in every all-AI batch since slice E: completing the victory
contract is slice 17's work). The Expert seat is not yet better than a Hard seat: at AD
2300 the Expert seat (seat 0) understands a median of 194 technologies against 195 for
the Hard seat; it reaches orbital transport (T142) in 27 of 32 matches (median AD 2000)
against 25 for the Hard seat (median 1920); T167 was reached by 4 seats of 128 (one of them
the Expert seat, in 2430). doc 39 section 8.4 asks the Expert policy to win >= 80% with a
median win 2100-2200: far from met. The skeleton plays the critical path only in research
(75% of lines, a larger goal weight); education 30 years ahead, the launch complex,
station and yard as soon as their requirements are discovered, and integration from 1950
are still the Hard policy's. The paired smart-vs-naive test (ai_paired_choice) and the
64-seed ai_no_self_block / ai_route_kept tests of doc 39 section 3.5 were not run.
