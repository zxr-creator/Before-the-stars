all but a few, the rest subordinate) in most matches by 2000-2200; 22 seats held 96
months of continuous governance at some sample. T167 (fleet-wide jump standards) was

path is no longer the limit. Failed checks over the seat-decades after T167:

 supplied station with a shipyard) 71%, scientific base 47%, two assemblies in series
 43%, links and power at the sites 42%, stores of fuel 38%, replacement 37%, crews 23%,

 governance: food 34%, water 28%, the governing form 23%, a last region 9%.

hard win in eight (AD 2058, AD 2238, AD 2320 in three batches), and the perf seed

native seats: refined fuel (fields and power stations take it before the programme),
electricity and qualified technicians at the test-node regions, and food at the ceiling
of the land for populations of 0.5-1.2 billion.

Steps (8-seed batches b0-b31 on eval-0..7; each a whole match)
1. Site power deadlock (the largest single cause of job 3's stagnation): generator halls
   took all spare shaft work, so the AI saw none for a site and ordered nothing industrial
   from about 1800 (a 70 M seat stood at 138 technologies for 400 years); coal pits held
   back by power stopped coal planning. Fixed in the AI (protocol 2.6 shows the halls'
   share). Industrial seats then reached 150-330 M.
2. Pre-modern brake: the land factor's neutral point 4,800 -> 6,300 bp of carrying
   capacity (the half of the seats stuck at 1-3 M grew); 7,000 overshot by 3-4x, 6,000
   left antiquity low; raider budding ends at AD 1300.
3. A small lasting food shortfall grew the nutrition debt without bound (hunger deaths
   of 1.5% a year at 98% access): the debt now settles.
4. Education, research, administration scaled with the people (the AI trained a
   thousandth of what modern works need; research capacity was 0.07% of the people);
   programmes no longer flip every round and use most of the action budget.
5. Integration from 1850 (doc 36 section 7): wars and subordination by the hard seat;
   offices for the regions it takes (stability had fallen to 5 at 51 regions).
6. The critical path (doc 39 section 8.5): critical tier 7-9 work 50/35/30%, leads at
   50/40%, T162-T167 30 minimum months; T167 moved from never to median 2060.
7. Programme installations founded and supplied by the AI; check-13 and series rules made
   consistent with their text; lattice works at 40 a month (three at 20 could never meet
   check 10).
8. Era prosperity in the development factor so that growth slows after 2000.
9. Round cost: migration and conversions recomputed block totals many times a move.
