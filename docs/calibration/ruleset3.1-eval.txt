Known gaps
 - The hard opponent does not win more often than the medium ones (9 of 32 against 22):
   in about a third of matches it reaches the scientific method centuries after a medium
   neighbour. Its research and era-entry rules were tuned for ruleset 3.0 and were not
   retuned; changing its research split alone made no difference on 16 seeds.
 - The easy opponent rarely leaves the Middle Ages (about sixty technologies at the end).
 - The spread between seats is wide: the scientific method arrives between AD 1300 and
   1780 for the middle half of seats, and a quarter of seats never enter the Early Modern era.
 - Early Modern entry is late against its window (median 1750 against 1400-1600) while
   the eras after it are in or near theirs.
 - Population of the median seat: 3.5 M in AD 1000, 23 M in 1800, 70 M in 2000 (below
   design table 3.1 after 1800); the largest seats reach 400-800 M.
 - Supremacy never decided a match in these batches: the native policy no longer goes to
   war for the objective itself.
