## 1. Reading conventions and numeric responsibilities

**Kinds:** `S` is source state that can change future outcomes and must be checkpointed. `F` is fixed at initialization/founding, with version and instance preserved. `D` is derived from explicit sources/windows; caching cannot create a second authoritative ledger. `O` is an actually received observation, preserving its historical content rather than following later truth. Actions, signatures, and occurred events are source state. Window-derived values require underlying window records, not only the current scalar.

**Visibility:** referee-only means no direct seat output. Permissioned projection requires actual knowledge and authority, not omniscience over everything nominally domestic. Participants receive contract/organization information only after communication. Scenario-public means explicitly disclosed without revealing hidden content. Every observation also has provenance, date, scope, and revision. Nationality, property, actual control, executable authority, and knowledge are separate relations, not one `owner` field.

**Stages:** P1 environmental baseline, arrivals, received messages, effective rights, protected payables, lawful advances; P2 monthly reservations; P3 service network, production, maintenance, teaching, research; P4 markets/shipping; P5 military; P6 consumption, receipts, population/skills; P7 organizations, culture, engineering verification; P8 performance settlement, deadlines, consolidation, events. P0 is generation, D0 the decision boundary. The [monthly engine](07-monthly-engine-spec.md), [economy](../design/02-civilization-systems.md), and [contracts](../design/14-contract-resolution.md) determine substages. Each stage uses shared snapshots, resource arbitration, and stable commits; iteration order cannot allocate inputs.

Compute `raw_target = clamp(anchor + 5000 × s, 0, 10000)` and `gap=raw_target−current`. Directed gap is max(0, gap) when s>0, min(0, gap) when s<0, 0 when s=0; then `target=current+q×directed_gap`. Shock limits are 60 bp/month, 500 bp/year, at most 12 consecutive months; these are convergence rates, not fixed axis bonuses. Real migration may quickly change observed cultural composition; norm speed limits cannot erase incoming people.

## 14. Unique ledgers and derived indicators across systems

These are implementation acceptance responsibilities, not additional rewards:

1. **Unique people:** residents, travelers, service members, trainees, wounded, captives refer to one population lineage. Only actual births, deaths, and explicit external-world flows change world count. Citizenship, conversion, splitting, migration create nobody.
2. **Unique time:** teaching, research, farming, care, and combat share available hours. Graduation, cultural adoption, and engineering acceptance affect corresponding new tasks no earlier than next month.
3. **Unique assets:** inventory, reservation, transit, custody, and damage have unique batches/executable control. Claims, promises, budget ceilings, and valuations are not cash or spot goods.
4. **Unique consumption:** factories, armies, schools, and public services compete for the same energy, transport, materials. Nutrition recovery does not deduct food again; culture does not create another research output.
5. **Results differ from capability:** understanding, prototype, historical adoption, license, qualification, and current sustained operation are separate. Preserve learning while requiring real equipment/maintenance for future work.
6. **Continuous responsibility:** renamed, split, and merged projects/contracts/armies/polities preserve consumption, residues, debts, experience, and governance history. Actual completion/lawful discharge reduces responsibility; actual controlling-seat change resets its required window.
7. **Independent observations:** true population conservation differs from registration changes; registration corrections are separate. Hidden-truth-derived best bottlenecks or exact cultural explanations cannot masquerade as reports.

National GDP, average culture/education, total military strength, fiscal pressure, and technology layer may be **explicitly scoped presentation summaries**, not substitute source inputs for local relationships. A maximum education-spending share is only a statistic. The model reads teachers, students, hours, facilities, knowledge, actual income, and complements.
