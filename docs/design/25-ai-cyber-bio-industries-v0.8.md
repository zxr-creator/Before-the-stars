# 0.8: Concrete AI, Cybernetic, and Biological Work Packages

This chapter expands the [last three technology families](22-technology-strata-v0.7.md) into nine work packages using [existing products, facilities, and recipes](21-production-chains-v0.7.md). These are not nine new research points, and civilizations need not complete all of them. Numbers are reproducible candidate game content, not real training, clinical, or aerospace budgets. Future general agents, neural interfaces, regeneration, and extensive ecological closure remain **[Research]**. FTL remains a separately identified fictional endgame premise.

Packages describe prototyping, adaptation, verification, and deployment of known problems. Paying inputs proves work occurred, not guaranteed discovery. Material mismatch, data bias, validation failure, or rejected adoption can leave projects incomplete. Unexpanded upstream facilities, civil works, task equipment, and population supply are not free.

Within a content version, complete technology and culture graphs are identical every match. Seeds generate geography, resources, initial society, and encounters, not graph rewrites. Local discovery, usable applications, adoption order, and development direction vary. These packages belong to that fixed catalog; actual conditions, history, and actions determine discovery and value.

v1.0 retains these conditional engineering examples under exclusively competitive matches: four total civilizations, one to four external human/LLM seats, native AI filling the rest, and one possible winner. They establish no measured full-match outcome or universally best route.

## 1. What the three families provide

| Family | Actual advantages | Conditions it cannot replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI | Less manual work for defined tasks, prediction/scheduling, funded experiment throughput; candidates may handle broader delegated tasks | Appropriate data, physical experiments, hardware, energy, maintenance, independent review, political authority |
| Cybernetic infrastructure | Regional information coordination, traceable operation, closed-loop equipment control, verified assistance, service networks | Coverage, terminals/personnel, delays, data rights, recovery, cooperating participants |
| Biological industry | Qualified feedstock into specified food/material/medical products, health or reduced supplies, research into closed habitats | Feedstock/nutrients, water, cleanliness, energy, long observation, residual handling, population maturation |

AI need not hold supreme authority. Digital society need not universalize bodily augmentation. Biological factories can coexist with resupplied space life support. They help materials, orbit, and governance through concrete tasks, not magical FTL shortcuts. Even advanced capability may offer insufficient return when outside the current critical path.

## 2. Shared records

| Record | Required actual references |
| --- | --- |
| Problem/domain | Known task, site/equipment/language/population, baseline method, prior acceptance definition |
| Data/knowledge | Source process, collection month, training/validation use, access/copy rights, version, known bias/missingness |
| Capital/services | Hardware, device-hours, networks, stations, electricity/cooling/water, premises, maintenance |
| Personnel | Qualifications, attendance, supervision, teacher/student time, interpretation/review, replacements |
| Operation/results | Inputs, executable outputs, failures, rework, degradation, verified savings, outstanding tasks |
| Rights/dependencies | Manufacture, execution, teaching, sublicensing, model/strain/data rights, outsourcing interruption, exit duties |

A factory adopts one task version, not a nationwide recipe replacement after one acceptance. [Technology prerequisites](17-technology-atlas-v0.6.md) remain. [Prototype-to-teaching rules](19-education-and-transmission-v0.6.md) create the first teachable capabilities without circularly requiring graduates of nonexistent future courses.

## 3. Compute, data, and knowledge units

`CB(task_spec)` is a compatible computation batch for a specified benchmark version. Training, inference, planning, imaging, and biological design are separately accounted; identically named CB are not interchangeable 1:1. Only locally verified compatible hardware uses the chapter-21 example rate: 1 CB = 1 device-hour, total facility electricity 0.0028 MWh, cooling water 0.002 m³, maximum 400 qualified hours per machine per month.

Tasks specify maximum parallel devices, memory/bandwidth, separable stages, and synchronization overhead. Ten machines can serve ten independent jobs but cannot give fivefold speed to a two-device task. CB is budget or delivered work, not an intelligence score. Unvalidated output does not change production recipes.

Reading usually does not consume data/knowledge. Rights-holders may sell multiple nonexclusive licenses and public research may benefit several states. Each recipient still needs delivery, reading/training, and local adaptation. Mentor hours, laboratory equipment, runtime capacity, reserved power, physical strain batches, held collateral, and exclusive rights cannot be copied with documents.

Lawful model copies carry no extra compute. Many can learn strain-use knowledge, but new living/feedstock batches require actual cultivation, checks, and time. Digital versions, physical batches, and political population remain separate accounts.

## 4. AI: task models through high autonomy

### P-A1: Narrow prediction and assisted scheduling [Established]

Example: spare-parts demand and maintenance scheduling for one workshop, using actual orders, operation, and failure records. It is not automatically valid for another mine or equipment type. Related technologies: T113/T115/T116; staffing includes programming, statistics, process expertise, and verification.

| Stage | Explicit inputs | Delivery and earliest time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital/access | Actual use rights to two compatible HW plus available F07 room, storage, terminals. If newly built, two R022: EC 800 items, ME 60 kg, CU 20 kg, IN 10 kg, assembly electricity 0.4 MWh, assembly 40 h, verification 16 h | Rented existing positions can connect; new hardware becomes usable next month. Model projects create no free room |
| Data preparation | Two distinct-use datasets processed with R024: 40 CB, preparation 40 h, domain checks 16 h, plus actual field collection 160 h | At least one month; training and holdout source processes remain distinct |
| Training/adaptation | 2,000 CB, software 80 h, domain review 40 h; maximum two devices/800 CB monthly | Compute alone takes at least three months; produces an unaccepted task model |
| Independent validation/integration | 600 CB, independent specialist review 80 h, actual integration 160 h | At least two months after training, covering observed conditions excluded from training; six months total minimum, no activation after failure |
| Monthly operation | 100 CB, task work 40 h, independent monitoring/exception review 20 h | Up to 1,000 same-specification tasks; electricity 0.28 MWh, water 0.2 m³; unused service is not stored |

Construction-period 2,640 CB consumes 7.392 MWh and 5.28 m³, excluding new hardware and other collection inputs. Timing assumes all staff, storage, data, and equipment arrive. Money and extra compute cannot replace missing failure records.

### P-A2: Automated experiments and engineering-model collaboration [Established]

Two material-test stations, related to T114/T118/T121. Each gains ME 100 kg, EC 200 items, CU 10 kg, IN 5 kg in adapted actuators/components. Combined installation requires electromechanical V2 240 h, verification V3 120 h, and 2 MWh, taking at least six months. Stations, gauges, sample preparation, and safety facilities must already exist.

Each defined trial consumes structural sample 2 kg, known coating 0.1 kg, station electricity 0.05 MWh, and 16 station-hours. Its 2.1 kg specimen goes to records/disposal, not unused stock. The original method takes operation 12 h plus scientific review 4 h. The verified automated method substitutes supervision 4 h and 20 CB for operation; scientific review remains 4 h. There is still one physical experiment.

Each station supplies 192 hours monthly, so two support at most 24 trials, regardless of inference count. Full monthly operation needs 48 kg samples, 2.4 kg coating, 384 station-hours, supervision 96 h, scientific review 96 h, and 480 CB. Electricity is 1.2 MWh station plus 1.344 compute = 2.544 MWh, with actual cleaning/maintenance additional. Extra models queue when stations are full.

Initial adoption requires 36 independently recorded control trials over at least three months of declared conditions. After the six-month conversion, formal scale operation begins no earlier than month 10. Slicing one specimen does not create independent successes; independence follows source material/treatment. The verified eight-hour saving per trial is not an additional global AI multiplier.

### P-A3: Bounded adaptation and delegated trial of high-autonomy systems [Research]

Related candidates T122/T125 do not guarantee general-agent research success. First obtain a real candidate model/prototype with known tested capabilities. This package adapts/verifies deployment; 24,000 CB does not guarantee general intelligence. Independent development or legal acquisition is separately funded.

Adaptation budgets 24,000 CB, software/systems engineering 2,400 h, domain design/review 1,200 h. Maximum four compatible devices/1,600 CB monthly implies at least 15 compute months. Data preparation, interfaces, and isolation add at least three months, followed by at least twelve continuous months of shadow/limited-authority trials: minimum 30 months. New personnel training may determine a later finish.

Deployment has at least two genuinely distinct failure-domain units, each with two usable HW, power/storage, interfaces, and accountable takeover personnel. Copied weights do not provide independent judgment; heterogeneous provenance and shared dependencies are audited separately. Each unit reserves monthly 400 CB, domain supervision 40 h, and takeover/recovery drills 16 h. Total: 800 CB, 112 h, 2.24 MWh, 1.6 m³. Only unused capacity within each unit's 800-CB hardware ceiling can serve other tasks.

Remaining business operations need additional workers. In the procurement example below, 120 h remains, giving 232 h total rather than pretending 112 supervision hours perform every real task. People, pay, and positions need actual sources.

Only accepted task scopes may execute automatically, such as budget-capped orders under authorized procurement rules. No new taxation, sovereignty, or cross-organizational data rights appear. Supreme administration requires cultural practice/charter procedures, not high model scores. Upgrades/renaming do not reset governance windows. Correlated errors suspend affected powers while preserving executed transactions/debts, not rolling back the world. In-game systems perform authorized simulated work; they grant no extra external LLM calls, secret access, or player reasoning budget.

## 5. Cybernetics: networks, control, and bodily assistance

### P-C1: Four-node traceable coordination network [Established]

Related T112/T113/T115. Each of four terminals has qualified HW and identity/storage devices. New links need conductors 200 kg, insulation 20 kg, protective/mounting ME 100 kg. Actual route, distance, communication mode, and capacity must be instantiated; these inputs do not create an arbitrary-distance global network. Wiring/configuration 400 h and independent inspection 120 h require at least six construction months, then three actual cross-node trial months.

Four R028 batches process at most 4,000 specified messages/administrative tasks monthly: 200 CB, direct network power 0.2 MWh, maintenance/exception work 64 h. With 0.56 MWh compute, total is 0.76 MWh and compute cooling 0.4 m³. Spare HW capacity does not imply spare network or staff capacity.

Deliver records with source, authority, sending, and arrival times. Literacy/language, terminal access, and trust limit real use. A spreadsheet does not prove villages received food/water. Cross-civilization records can reduce checking cost if interfaces/evidence rules are agreed. Connection does not merge treasuries or reveal entire foreign databases.

### P-C2: Digital models and recovery control for two facilities [Established]

Related T114/T121/T123. Each of two industrial facilities adds EC 100 sensor/actuator items, ME 50 kg, and local control configuration. Combined electromechanical work 240 h, software/data work 240 h, independent verification 160 h, installation electricity 1 MWh. Calibration needs 2,400 CB plus 48 real field comparison tasks of at least four qualified operation/measurement hours each; self-generated model records cannot substitute. Minimum conversion six months, then field validation six months.

Monthly combined needs: 400 CB, direct controls 0.1 MWh, electromechanical maintenance 24 h, independent checks/recovery drills 24 h. Total at least 1.22 MWh and water 0.8 m³, plus original production inputs. Only validated warning/control/maintenance tasks change scheduling. Forecast avoided accidents cannot be monetized as fictitious revenue.

During disconnection, facilities execute only acquired still-valid local authority and retain facts awaiting synchronization. They cannot instantly query the referee. Synchronization must prevent duplicate budgets/asset dispositions. Isolated replicas may be read-only or restricted to reserved tasks. Reconnection merges unique processes/liability, not both branches' complete gains.

### P-C3: Medical assistance and neural-interface candidates [Established/Research separated]

Verified T119 assistive devices may be applied before high-bandwidth T120 interfaces, which remain distinct research candidates. Installing the former does not complete the latter. This example concerns eight real participants and one specified task: eight accepted adapted devices, two measurement/computing HW, appropriate care/maintenance facilities. Each device needs a real product specification/source; a generic EC module is not a complete device.

Local adaptation of verified devices takes at least six months, medical-engineering work 480 h, individual measurement 160 h, 1,200 CB, and actual consumables. High-bandwidth candidates additionally require separate research and at least 24 months of task/failure observation. This is a game content minimum, not a real clinical-sufficiency claim. Failure may prevent deployment indefinitely.

Monthly ongoing inputs for eight participants: their own practice/examination 32 h, medical care 32 h, engineering maintenance 16 h, 100 CB, direct device power 0.2 MWh, and specified external maintenance material 1 kg; compute adds 0.28 MWh. Outputs are these individuals' verified assistance and continuing duties, not population-wide intelligence, skill uploads, or eight extra people. Consent, care, exit, and ownership have actual institutional records. Copying plans copies neither bodily adaptation nor observation history.

## 6. Biological industry: products, design, and habitat cycles

### P-B1: Stable manufacture of a specified biological product [Established]

Related T131/T132, requiring an actual F09 and feedstock-processing facilities. Chapter 21 supplies F09 civil works, reactor, instruments, eighteen-month construction, and quality staff. Strain information and real use rights are separate. Adoption needs at least three independent qualified batches over at least three months. With three successes, verified operation begins no earlier than month 22; trial success is not guaranteed.

R029 + R030 per batch consumes raw qualified dry material 120 kg, process water 3 m³, electricity 0.25 MWh, heat 1 GJ, filter material 2 kg, targeting BP 30 kg dry matter. Residual feed, metabolic equivalents, water, and gases retain recipe accounting. Product identity is fixed before the batch; unsold material cannot be renamed food. If each of three acceptance batches consumes 1 kg for testing, target output 90 leaves only 87 kg usable in qualified applications.

At twenty batches/month, F09 needs 2,400 kg raw inputs, 60 m³ water, 5 MWh, 20 GJ, and 40 kg filters for target 600 kg output. Output is not a factory-level bonus. Combined R029/R030 operation is 480 h, quality testing 240 h, and fixed F09 maintenance 40 h: at least 760 h. Preparation and reaction stations need respectively 160/480 hours. More reactors cannot replace missing quality staff or nutrient feed.

Feedstock may be inedible byproducts or compete with food, husbandry, and land. Opportunity cost follows composition/use. Turning 120 kg edible input into 30 kg valuable product can be rational without adding 30 kg net food. Nutrition, palatability, medicines, and structural material quality are distinct; kilograms are not a universal welfare unit.

### P-B2: Biological design and long-duration validation [Established/Research separated]

T134 may use twelve R031 batches: 1,200 CB, research 288 h, independent review 144 h, compute electricity 3.36 MWh, water 2.4 m³. Then conduct twelve physical product/environment batches under R030 or a specified field recipe. R030 requires at least BF 1,200 kg, water 24 m³, process electricity 2.4 MWh, heat 6 GJ, filters 24 kg, plus quality/operation hours.

An industrial instance covers at least three actual sites/input sources and twelve observation months. Triplicate copies are not three-site evidence. A variant may raise qualified output from 30 to 35 kg per batch under validated feedstock, only if measurements support it and corresponding residuals/other inputs change. Twelve trials do not force a bonus or universal feedstock advantage.

T136 regeneration/augmentation medicine is a separate instance requiring T119, clinical personnel, actual care, and long records. Industrial twelve-month validation does not qualify every medical use. This version's candidates require at least 24 months of applicable observation/individual follow-up. Scope, failure states, and recurring inputs must be separately frozen. Until defined, T136 remains a research opportunity, not a vague health bonus in production.

### P-B3: Modular recovery and high-closure habitat trials [Established/Research separated]

Conventional T144 life support permits actual resupply. T135/T137 high-closure trials are optional depth. Start with qualified F10/R033 water recovery: monthly wastewater 10 m³, electricity 0.2 MWh, filters 2 kg → target water 9.5 m³, concentrate 0.5 m³, spent filters. Operation 8 h and inspection 4 h plus F10 fixed maintenance 16 h and parts 2 kg. Outputs enter stock next month. Startup therefore requires potable-water buffer covering at least one processing cycle; water cannot simultaneously be available stock and consumed input.

High-closure habitats additionally require actual habitation, food/air/oxygen demand tables, unrecovered nutrient resupply, heat disposal, and buffers. At least one inhabited unit operates 24 months, followed by twelve months covering replacement crews and recovery tasks. The 36-month minimum is a content floor, not automatic acceptance. Capacity follows actual joint service bottlenecks, not population multiplied by a closure percentage.

This water loop is not a complete ecosystem. Without food, gas, and waste instances, operation remains resupplied T144. Ninety-five-percent water recovery cannot claim no resupply, full T135, or final FTL life-support acceptance. Freed transport becomes useful only with actual scheduling, cargo, and payment.

| Course path | Real baseline for a qualified incoming class | Industrial consequence |

| E020 biological/medical/ecological, eight | Chosen known domain and R3/N3; 36 months, teachers 72 h/month, students 960 h/month, field/lab 320 h | One specialty tag only; food inspectors are not automatically clinical/orbital ecologists |
| E023 orbital life support, eight | Adopted T144 and role equipment competence; 36 months, teachers 80 h/month, students 960 h/month, habitat 384 hours | Resupplied and high-closure specialties differ; high closure is optional |

These durations follow entry prerequisites, not childhood-to-scientist time. Actual equivalent skills may qualify through task records; renaming credentials cannot skip them. Teachers, students, stations, inference, and research compete in one ledger.

Foreign mentors can advance a critical path by years, a legitimate gain. They supply only available hours, and their home civilization loses service or seeks compensation. Educational access, occupational esteem, language, religious permissions, household care, and income support affect actual enrollment/retention. Culture works through participation, not extra hours or altered energy units.

## 8. Two economic choices without a predetermined winner

**Task AI's scale threshold.** For a verified P-A1 service, suppose rented compatible compute includes hardware, room, and operation at 0.2 c/CB. Software/operation costs 2 c/h; domain review 3 c/h. With existing premises/terminals, the buyer needs preparation/collection 200 ordinary hours, 16 specialist hours, 40 CB; training 80 ordinary, 40 specialist, 2,000 CB; validation 80 specialist, 600 CB; integration 160 ordinary hours. Totals: ordinary 440, specialist 136, compute 2,640, costing `880 + 408 + 528 = 1,816 c`. An explicit nonexclusive license adds 184, total 2,000. Compute pricing includes power/water/equipment; do not charge electricity twice in cash, although the physical ledger records 7.392 MWh and 5.28 m³.

At verified equal quality, the old method uses 120 h/month costing 240 c. AI uses operation 40 h, specialist monitoring 20 h, and 100 CB, costing `80 + 60 + 20 = 160 c`, saving 80. Sustained equal demand needs 25 operating months to recover 2,000 c, before considering outages/task changes. At one-tenth volume, humans use 12 h/24 c while AI uses operation 4 h, the still-required monitoring 20 h, and 10 CB: `8 + 60 + 2 = 70 c`, more expensive. Fixed monitoring is this validated use's condition, not a global anti-AI penalty.

**Water recovery's logistics threshold.** For one monthly R033 batch with equal water quality and available disposal, assume power 5 c/MWh, V2 operation 2 c/h, V3 checks 3 c/h, filters 4 c/kg, parts 5 c/kg. Monthly cost: power 1, operation/checks 28, filters 8, fixed maintenance 32, parts 10 = 79 c. It recovers 9.5 m³ and still buys 0.5. At delivered water 20 c/m³, direct 10 m³ costs 200; recovery costs `79 + 0.5×20 = 89`, saving 111 monthly. At water 2 c/m³, direct costs 20 and recovery 80, an extra 60. Construction, transport, and finance remain additional; operating differences are not total profit. Larger loads may spread maintenance but still require equipment, wastewater, and staff.

AI/biology need not pay everywhere, and routes need not have equal returns. Price, scale, demand, logistics, assets, skills, validation, and dependence determine choices. A scarce specialist, license, or access right relieving a genuine bottleneck can yield returns far beyond its immediate price.

## 9. Task-level gains and rework

Before adoption, record the baseline's actual time, qualified output, errors/rework, location, and coverage for the same task. Afterward, compare completed work and every added input. Saved labor can be reassigned only after the actual people are released. Employer wage savings are not simultaneously treasury savings and free new labor; reassigned workers may still receive wages.

The following are predefined local test cases, not guaranteed effects of industry names. Without comparable evidence, report actual work and uncertainty, not omniscient avoided loss.

| Package | Baseline/task | Measurable gains and retained costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P-A1 | 1,000 same-specification monthly requests; qualified human method 120 h | Operation 40 h + monitoring 20 h releases 60 h; compute/utilities/specialist roles increase; cash above |
| P-A2 | 24 trials, each human operation 12 h + science 4 h | Human 384 h versus verified automation 192 h releases 192 h; samples, 384 station-hours, science remain, plus 480 CB |
| P-A3 | Real procurement domain, baseline 400 h/month, equal predefined delivery/error tolerances | Business 120 h + supervision/recovery 112 = 232; releases only 168 h, with no other-use or sovereignty expansion |
| P-C1 | 4,000 traceable records, old handling/checking 160 h; original collection separately paid in both | Network/exception work 64 h can release 96; disconnected paper work remains; message count is not service provision |
| P-C2 | Same maintenance work across two facilities, baseline 400 h | If field comparison supports business 300 plus control/checks 48, releases 52; no monetized hypothetical accidents |
| P-C3 | Eight people performing a registered functional task; baseline independence/time/helpers/quality | Report individual capacity and total care/maintenance. Autonomy can improve while total social hours rise; no forced positive labor gain or population intelligence bonus |
| P-B1 | Actual demand and same-quality existing purchase/recipe with real price/transport/capacity | Target 30 kg/batch with all inputs. Without comparable old method, report new capability, not invented savings; edible inputs retain food opportunity cost |
| P-B2 | Twelve independent trials at three sites, two old/two candidate per site; old 30 kg, candidate 35 only if measured | Six old = 180, six candidate = 210, total 390. Later twelve all-candidate batches may give 420 versus 360, +60 with matching residual reduction |
| P-B3 | Same 10 m³ demand/quality, reachable outside supply | Actual recovered 9.5 plus external 0.5, with labor/power/filters/maintenance; only truly freed transport serves other cargo |

**Recomputable failure:** P-A2 performs 24 physical trials, but independent checks find timing bias in eight outside agreed tolerance. Only sixteen reports qualify. All materials, energy, compute, and labor were consumed. Eight failed records remain and cannot be renamed successes.

After correction/revalidation, repeating eight requires sample 16 kg, coating 0.8 kg, station 128 h, supervision 32 h, science 32 h, 160 CB; electricity 0.4 trial + 0.448 compute = 0.848 MWh. If next month's stations already schedule 24 trials, repeats displace eight new ones rather than creating 32. Diagnosis, code changes, and difference validation also cost known tasks; rework materials do not freely fix every cause.

Out-of-domain mismatch follows the same logic. A workshop model moved to an untested mine initially provides scoped suggestions while automated authority remains paused; human work and paid maintenance continue. More inference cannot turn an unknown error rate into known accuracy. Follow-up/continuous operation uses real simulation months. Parallel compute can analyze more received records, not compress twelve months of new observation into one.

Before execution, each instance must freeze task generation scope, baseline, capability/error response, tolerance, measurement noise, correlated sources, and mismatch triggers. Participants see only delivered samples/measurements/reports. Undefined packages remain candidates; narrative models cannot improvise “AI +30%,” and response tables cannot change because one civilization leads.

## 10. Validation, correlated errors, and partial failure

Validation records distinguish model/program version, training sources, collection process, physical batch, sensors/calibration, power/network failure domains, and review institution. Two institutions relaying one measurement are not two experiments. Shared weights/data/calibration cannot count as independent evidence. Different names or suppliers do not prove independence.

Deployment validity needs actual evidence not used for the current adjustment and reviewers with real permission/competence. Samples disclosed for development remain knowledge but require fresh process records for subsequent independent acceptance. Repeatedly selecting one favorable score is prohibited. The game borrows reproducibility and deployment-context principles, not institutional compliance scores. [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/).

| Failure | Immediate response | Retained value/responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing data rights/delivery | Pause restricted stages; lawful work may continue | Delivered information, paid services, outstanding duties, dispute scope remain distinct |
| Model mismatch at new equipment/site | Narrow scope, restore available manual process or adapt | Original use does not automatically fail; new data/errors remain recorded |
| Correlated multisystem error | Suspend affected tasks, use actual backups/people | Identical copies do not pretend redundancy; executed orders/losses do not roll back |
| Failed biological quality | Quarantine/rework/dispose, never sell as qualified BP | Feedstock/time consumed; recovery needs another real recipe |
| Closed habitat lacks supply/heat disposal | Recognize only working subsystems; use real backup or end occupancy | Water-recovery knowledge remains, without full ecology/population creation |
| Supplier breach/local exit | Apply contracts/authority to future services, rearrange available assets | Knowledge remains, assets do not teleport, debts/resettlement/maintenance duties persist |

NASA life-support descriptions separately address water recovery, air revitalization, and oxygen. This supports separate subsystem accounting, not the game's 95% or 36-month parameters. [NASA ECLSS](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/).

## 11. Prevent resource farming through advanced systems

- Nonexclusive knowledge may benefit many; exclusive rights, mentor slots, and HW occupancy cannot be promised twice. Audit rights and physical capacity separately.
- A real machine, digital twin, dashboard, and model refer to one disposable inventory. Simulated outputs never enter physical stores.
- Interface simulation, shadow mode, and real execution are separate. Registering one command in all three cannot triple production, wages, or taxes.
- Training/inference share actual CB/device-hours. Pausing preserves only saved checkpoints. Recreated jobs grant no extra progress or new draws for the same trial.
- Samples, reports, models, and strains can have new legitimate uses. Renaming creates neither independent evidence nor repeated research/culture reward.
- Automated procurement, administration, and military agents obey existing budgets/authority. Successful tool invocation proves neither delivery nor acquired political power.
- Recovery retains losses, quality, temperature, nutrients, and waste. Every modeled component has a source; one residual cannot simultaneously become food, material, and fertilizer.
- Advanced devices do not remove demographic maturation, learning, or job adaptation. Actual health improvement may release labor through real state changes, not duplicate an augmented population.

Cash, idempotency, custody, and reward counterexamples follow [24](24-decision-value-and-exploit-boundaries-v0.7.md). This chapter does not prove that no AI, cybernetic, or biological strategy dominates across scenarios. Compare packages within real demand, finite supply, education, and competitive contracts. Each executable instance still needs complete equipment, samples, provenance, prices, and behavior parameters. Undefined advanced capabilities remain research.
