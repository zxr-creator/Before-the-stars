## 1. From technology to actual capability

| Object | Mandatory content | Cannot be replaced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability/knowledge | Evidence, understood scope, prototype, process version | Technology/style/era name |
| Product specification | Material, quality, tolerance, unit, location, available month | Aggregated resource-family quantity |
| Recipe | Inputs, outputs, residues, energy grade, task/equipment hours | Research spending or industry points |
| Capital project | Equipment list, civil works, duration, builders/inspectors, trials | Immediate full-output factory after payment |
| Operating network | Fuel/water/power, transport, repairs, stocks, lawful dispatch, demand | Installed rating or nominal stock |
| Qualified population | Task credentials, available hours, practice, authorization maintenance | Average education or cultural multiplier |
| Economy/rights | Cash, assets, licenses, continuing duties, real counterparties | Resource value automatically transferred to treasury |

A substitute must supply the same accepted function, not merely claim lower cost. Steam shafts can drive generators with suitable speed, coupling, regulation, conductors, insulation, and electricians. One shaft flow cannot also fully drive tools. Diesel, steam, and water have different fuel/geographic costs but can deliver qualified electricity. Electricity does not itself establish electronics manufacture.

Performance determines substitution. Ordinary metal, pressure material, conductors, pure substrates, and space materials remain distinct. Wood, ceramic, or natural insulation may work where qualified, without all motors waiting for petroleum. Advanced clean equipment needs lower-specification tools, imports, or explicit manual bootstrapping; a factory cannot make its own founding equipment before it exists.

Reciprocating generation has historical examples; electrolysis does not scientifically require a national grid. Fertilizers require specific nitrogen/hydrogen/nutrient sources and processes, not universal polymers. These sources inform capability relationships, not game prices, times, or efficiency. [Smithsonian Pearl Street station](https://www.si.edu/object/model-edisons-pearl-street-power-station%3Anmah_712587), [US Department of Energy electrolysis](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/hydrogen-production-electrolysis), [EPA synthetic-ammonia process](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/documents/8.1_synthetic_ammonia.pdf).

Weight-driven escapement clocks also provide a real alternative, so waterwheels are not the sole timekeeping prerequisite. [NIST history of timekeeping](https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/popular-links/walk-through-time/walk-through-time-revolution).

## 3. Reading the paired comparison

Review 06 copies one industrial checkpoint into three states over the same 120-month calendar, prioritizing steam, diesel, or early electricity. It is not a complete primitive-start world: prototypes, teachers, bounded suppliers/buyers are explicit initial conditions. Basic livelihoods/costs are common external controls. Compare monthly cash, actual components, unsold stock, qualifications, conversion downtime, and the date of 12 consecutive months delivering a common industrial capability.

The common endpoint is not FTL and changes no victory condition. All three supply minimum components, dedicated electricity, metrology, and maintenance staff. Complete victory still requires [15's integration, fleet FTL, and trials](15-victory-and-ftl.md). The 12-month industrial audit cannot replace G96/F24, and an electrical workshop 12 months early does not prove victory 12 months early.

Two conclusions can coexist: one starting condition may have a clearly superior choice; no universal best policy across maps, demand, knowledge, contracts, and unknown shocks has been established. If diesel mobility receives no orders in a stationary-shop test, do not add mobility points to make it win. Build a paired dispersed-demand test instead. Likewise, electricity's advantage is invalid if waterworks, conductors, or staff were free.

Adapting to bottlenecks is a policy family, not an optimal button. Seats use acquired evidence to judge demand, training lead times, maintenance, license risk, conversion timing. Unknown shocks cannot justify hindsight-optimal backup. Sensitivity analysis distinguishes original commitments from later informed changes, respecting the sealed decision calendar.

## 4. Actual late-stage AI, cyber, and bio choices

**Compute first or data first.** The small model instance in 21 needs 2,000 compatible CB. Each uses one hardware-hour and total electricity 0.0028 MWh, so training uses 5.6 MWh. One 400-hour/month module needs at least five months if entirely devoted to training. A prior 100 CB/month inference commitment leaves 300, extending compute to at least seven months. Data, rights, specialists, holdouts, storage, upkeep remain separate; these are compute lower bounds, not guaranteed success or full project duration.

More modules can shorten some scheduling, but projects must declare parallelism, interconnect, and memory. Ten incompatible machines do not yield tenfold throughput. Outsourcing buys actual services with communication, data rights, supplier capacity, and interruption costs. Only validated scoped tasks enter production; AI does not directly multiply national science.

**Automate experiments or expand instruments.** Automation can reduce only demonstrated replaceable manual tasks. One sample station cannot execute two physical experiments because inference increased. Devices produced at month end become assembly inputs next month; completed computers supply compute the following month. Pipelines may overlap, but newly made chips cannot train a model and retroactively validate research in the same month.

**Build networks or transfer authority.** Networks, terminals, databases, and actual administrative chains are technical investments. Public ownership, corporate jurisdictions, or highest AI authority are separate delegations. Responsibility, appeals, takeover, and data rights need actual organizations. R028 message/task counts do not automatically create taxes, consent, diplomatic credibility, or sovereignty.

**Closed biology or more resupply.** R033 consumes 10 m³ wastewater, 0.2 MWh, and filters to deliver 9.5 m³ accepted water. It reduces supply needs but leaves 0.5 m³ and treatment residues. Biological foods/materials/drugs have separate recipes and grades. More orbital resupply and higher recovery are both possible; freight, reliability, talent, resources, and duration determine value. High-closure ecology is not compulsory.

## 5. Culture, education, and institutions in these chains

Culture/religion do not alter heating values or elemental accounting. They affect who studies, entry permissions, trust, job prestige, and who bears losses. Education converts known content into task qualifications; qualified people still need jobs, equipment, pay, and hours. These change actual participation/inputs, not an additional multiplier after the production bottleneck.

Excluding foreign teachers reduces teaching supply. Stable apprenticeships may reduce departure while obstructing reassignment. Factions accepting conversion and credible income/retraining arrangements can retain old talent in new industry. Announcing reform creates no graduates. Founding doctrine stays fixed while schools, assets, adherents, and interpretations change; one religion does not impose one industrial attitude on everybody.

Long tendencies follow 20's attribution, source deduplication, direction limits, and drift caps. Short-term attendance, contracts, or teaching stoppage follow actual authority, return, and risk. One rejected teaching contract cannot instantly change national openness. Counterfactuals first change one organization or service and observe downstream effects while preserving other initial conditions.

## 6. Human and agent production/conversion panels

| Panel | Identically authorized information | Decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known processes | Obtained recipes, grades, batches, errors, quotes, source dates | Prototype, inspect, adopt, license, commission replication |
| Current chain | Registered stock, transit/pending acceptance, contracted services, vacancies, actual stoppage causes | Recipes, orders, stock targets, production priorities |
| Conversion | Reusable assets, known gap tasks, stages, expected shutdown, continuing obligations | Retrofit, new build, staging, parallel operation, shutdown/retirement |
| Talent/education | Verified distributions, places, teacher/station time, learning, graduation ranges | Recruit, retrain, import teachers, apprenticeships, receiving jobs |
| Energy/logistics | Measured deliveries, restrictions, stocks, transport, source risks | Energy mix, reserves, backup, rerouting, long contracts |
| Cash/risk | Cash, paid/due amounts, actual sales, assets, scenario forecasts/assumptions | Payments, financing, escrow, joint investment, optional budgets |

Provide sourced statements such as a 1 MWh shortfall against a signed supply or six learners completing eighteen effective months. Do not expose future full graphs, true culture, or hidden optimal-route judgments. Forecasts state known assumptions/ranges and cannot use omniscient weather or opponents' private inventories. Separate authorized budget, payment, delivered service, and accepted result so appropriation changes are not mistaken for scientific feedback.
