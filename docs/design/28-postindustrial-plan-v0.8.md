## 0. Fixed complete graphs, dynamic local knowledge and direction

Within one `content_version`, every match has exactly the same technology/practice nodes, AND/OR dependencies, alternatives, discovery rules, and economic/capability prerequisites. Versioned content defines them. Seeds cannot add/remove nodes, delete OR routes, rewire dependencies, or alter the final scientific premise. Graph changes require a content update, not match-level randomization.

Four state layers produce dynamism:

| Layer | Changes with match state? | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete technology/culture graph | Fixed within version | Electromagnetism, apprenticeship, public education, open research, and prerequisites always exist |
| Local implementation conditions | Yes, by fixed rules | Coal/water/conductors, equipment, qualified people, demand, property, religious organizations, cultural populations |
| Civilization's known graph | Expands through contact/evidence | Mine drainage reveals pressure-mechanics problems; imported motors reveal another direction earlier |
| Actual adoption and cultural population networks | Changes with investment, participation, interests, generations | Apprenticeship grows, open research is trialed, a region becomes closed; the root practice is not deleted |

Specialization accumulates equipment, experts, failure records, and markets, creating adjacent problems and lower actual transition burdens. It earns no separate preferred-route research multiplier. Geography changes available inputs. Religion affects access through education, participation rights, and institutions. Culture affects study, occupations, exchange, and reform. Technology/economy changes feasible practice. Fixed discovery rules read these states, yielding different visible nodes, leads, and practical difficulty.

Bias must not become an irreversible class lock. Qualified cross-domain experiments, exploration, legal teaching, and trade can open weak directions. Missing resources permit waiting, substitution, or transport investment. A hidden preference score cannot veto an experiment possessing every actual prerequisite. Undiscovered, not understood, missing input, missing rights, and not adopted are distinct states, not one gray button.

For example, A invests in coal drainage/pressure machinery and sees boiler, heat, and machine-tool problems first. B invests in hydro, conductors, and instruments and sees generation/control earlier. Buying B's plans and mentors reveals the same electrical nodes for A, while local training and installation remain necessary. Both can encounter public education/open research; adoption depends on teachers, household income, organizational permission, and credible experience. A fixed graph does not imply synchronized adoption.

The interface shows known nodes, evidence, and observed obstacles, never the full graph, private axes/weights, or omniscient shortest path. Concealment is not the only challenge: a model familiar with the catalog still lacks local deposits, measurements, rival intentions, and future performance. Separate first-exploration from informed strategy evaluation; isolation does not erase general-model memory.

## 1. Late capabilities perform tasks, not civilization multipliers

| Family | Actual capabilities | Continuing constraints | Alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI | Task models, processing, validated screening/scheduling, automated experiments, bounded autonomy | Compatible compute, data rights, hardware, energy, human/physical validation, error handling | Human specialists, ordinary software, outsourced models or narrow services |
| Cyber systems | Connected records, production/logistics interfaces, assistance, administrative execution/recovery | Terminals, bandwidth, maintenance, rights, local access, fallback, actual service institutions | Local systems, narrower networks, human authorization, offline backups |
| Biological industry | Defined food/material/medical production, samples/design, recovery, life-support subsystems | Feedstock, nutrients, water, reactors, testing, real observation time, waste | Existing manufacture, conventional health/supply, resupply, lower recovery |

Passing a task changes only the steps, inputs, or feasible scope it actually replaces: fewer screening hours, fewer invalid trials, or more delivered tasks on specified equipment. Do not multiply bottleneck-calculated output again by “AI research +50%.” Without demand, follow-up experiments, or operators, extra suggestions are not economic growth.

Known science stays unchanged. Highly autonomous systems, high-bandwidth neural interfaces, reliable regeneration, and extensive closed ecology remain research extrapolations where unverified. FTL is an explicit fictional premise. AI rule, a particular cyber property regime, and complete ecology closure are not universal FTL prerequisites.

## 2. Routes, people, and diplomacy share one critical path

The historical checkpoint has Riverland, Clearbay, Greenreach, and Farcape with differing assets, people, and information, not immutable ethnic technological gifts. Thermal/material, electrical/computing, biological/water, and precision/transport capabilities can complement one another or be substituted through costly transitions.

The archived main campaign was cooperative, checking whether shared engineering/governance could fit accounts, hours, and windows. v1.0 preserves its numbers as conditional engineering only. Beneficial technology exchange does not establish willingness to surrender ultimate sovereignty. The new competitive case is in [33](33-competition-and-native-ai-v1.0.md); no rules change near the ending.

Separate three levels of claims:

1. **Contemporaneously knowable:** own data, received quotes, public needs, actual failures, investigations; no hindsight planning from the future trajectory.
2. **Recomputable conditional outcomes:** given packages, supplies, personnel, and contractual performance, which stage completes when, costs what, and burdens whom.
3. **Full-engine outcomes still unknown:** whether endogenous households, markets, disease, warfare, and politics continue satisfying those conditions.

The case primarily checks level two while constraining narrative by level one. It is not proof of level-three success. Basic needs and unexpanded industries remain explicit finite boundary conditions; freezing them cannot validate the entire macroeconomy.

## 3. Large decision gains must relieve real bottlenecks

| Decision | Potential major benefit | Audit requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Train instrumentation, electrical, orbital, and specialist personnel early | Avoid another 24/36/48-month course wait | Show personnel on the critical path; if equipment arrives later, course length is not automatically time saved |
| Buy broad rights, mentoring, local verification | Avoid duplicate R&D and retain operation/improvement | Separate rights, information, learning, production; seller needs actual gain or motive |
| Validated AI screening | Save limited labor, samples, or stations | Independent outcomes, data/inference/review costs; wrong screening also causes losses |
| Common standards/interfaces early | Reduce repeated equipment/course/spares adaptation | Real adopters, transition cost, versions, maintenance; names grant no compatibility |
| Appropriate recovery instead of absolute self-sufficiency | Reduce transport while retaining affordable resupply | Account separately for water, gases, food, nutrients, heat; partial recovery is not complete closure |
| Actual global governance early | Overlap G96 with final engineering | Real powers, execution, resident services; signatures create no past governance months |

No extra policy reward is needed. Earlier capability, residual resources, and lower failure risk can already create large differences. Prices, knowledge, and diplomacy change investment order. Clearly superior choices under particular conditions are allowed.

## 4. Late-game exploits to prevent

- Pledging the same compute, mentor hours, equipment, or component to multiple simultaneous projects and accepting all in full.
- Copying datasets, model names, or reports to fake independent evidence; passing training data or referee truth as fresh validation.
- Retaining deliveries while repeatedly refunding settled licenses; refunding consumed joint-research budgets after failure.
- Treating water/material cycles or model copies as new elements, energy, population, or maintenance-free perpetual capacity.
- Renaming one production line as two; borrowing ultimate power at month end, backdating return, and inheriting invalid G96.
- Adding scoring identities through companies, AI copies, or government names; switching competition into shared victory midmatch.

Legitimate nonexclusive knowledge copying is allowed. One process helping two states or one contract saving years is intended value. Model capability does not grant sovereignty, and lawful authority does not supply missing data, hardware, or skills.

Ordinary verifiers act on evidence they possess. The engine's true provenance and conservation rules do not mean every organization detects deception for free. Liability, public reports, and actual technical performance remain separate, preventing anti-exploit interfaces from becoming foreign-information oracles.

## 5. Endgame time semantics

Historical milestones: endpoint revelation M145, qualified prototype M192, specialist training M193–240, governance from M241. Final checks include sustained military-wide capability, two independent lines, three-node tasks, component/crew replacement, maintenance, and two trials. [Campaign 07](../reviews/07-postindustrial-campaign-v0.8.md) specifies detailed inputs/dates.

Under its stated conditions, M241–336 contains 96 governance months, M313–336 contains 24 capability months, and M313/M325 trials are 12 months apart within valid governance. Count endpoints inclusively. Components complete this month and become usable next month; a component finished M325 cannot retroactively perform that month's completed trial.

Distinguish continuous monthly capability from cumulative manufacture of two qualified components within the prescribed window. Earlier capability can use an existing prototype only if it genuinely has mission capacity and maintenance. Future manufacture cannot fill past shortfalls. Funded next-twelve-month support is a future commitment, not elapsed twelve-month performance.

These dates belong only to the separate postindustrial checkpoint. They do not compress primitive-to-interstellar development into the same three hundred months or report a model's measured match result.

## 6. Experience and evaluation boundaries

[The walkthrough](27-first-game-mental-model-v0.8.md) begins without prior-match memory: address visible supply/process problems, connect towns and villages, establish scientific method, expand through education/trade, and discover the endpoint from later evidence. Each round observes, investigates, negotiates, and seals actual plans while projects, society, and opponents develop monthly.

Empty memory does not remove scientific priors. Exploration controls local information, action eligibility, and projections; full designer documents stay outside seat workspaces. External GPT/Claude calls are separate from in-game AI hardware/compute/model assets. Researching AI grants no free external inference budget.

Feedback includes real shortages, effective instruction, packages, acceptance, money, nondelivery, and prediction error. Formal reward remains complete victory time only. Intermediate technologies, alliance counts, and historical moments yield no farmable points. Historical cooperative accounting tests conditional coordination, not competitive win rate. Current competitive evaluation reports success, time, failure tails, and unresolved games with native AI policy/difficulty versions and equal information.

## 7. Deliverables and remaining work

This design added nine application packages, late licensing/verification semantics, a four-civilization historical case, twelve specific counterexamples, and a first-game mental model. Graphs, recipes, and written protocols still need complete content binding. No machine contract or backend has been implemented.

Remaining gates include task-specific model response/out-of-domain failures, defined biological products and long-observation acceptance, full upstream/capital lists, endogenous primitive-to-postindustrial economics, reproducible competitive negotiation/war policies, and full victory across seeds. Document arithmetic, dependencies, and window checks exclude specific counterexamples only; they do not prove absence of dominant strategies or all exploits.
