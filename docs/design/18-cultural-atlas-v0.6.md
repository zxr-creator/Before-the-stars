# 0.6 — Cultural Practices, Population Tendencies, and Interstellar Preparation

This complete initial catalog contains 64 practices and cognitive dependencies, eight cultural tendencies, institutional conflicts, interpretations of cultural evolution, and several ways to organize an interstellar society. Each match of one `content_version` uses identical nodes, AND/OR cognitive prerequisites, contact/discovery rules, and economic/technical/organizational adoption requirements. Candidates are methods populations or organizations can try, not a sampled subset. This is a designer atlas, not a starting player tree or implemented content package. Updates use [16's deduplication, evidence-strength scaling, and slow-change rules](16-knowledge-and-culture-engine.md); courses and abilities are in [19](19-education-and-transmission-v0.6.md). Version 1.0 [31](31-knowledge-and-culture-v1.0.md) clarifies dynamic presentation and revises C045/C047 below. [32](32-population-and-education-v1.0.md) supersedes excessive population detail. All matches are competitive; joint institutions do not award shared victory.

Like Civilization, recognizable practices and stages make cultural development legible. Unlike a spendable culture-point system, a government choice does not transform national beliefs, and societies need not converge on one political form. Technology enables execution; population experience, asset distribution, and bargaining determine adoption.

## Components of culture

| Layer | Stored content | Change and constraints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cognition | Whether a method has been encountered, understood, and formulated | Foreign examples, local proposals, written/oral teaching; knowledge is not acceptance |
| Practice | Who cooperates, learns, trades, inherits, allocates, or governs under which rules and where | Participation, authority, facilities, service, continuing performance; trial, scale, suspension, exit |
| Long-term tendencies | Eight bounded profile tendencies | Slowly updated through knowable experience and transmission; no direct goods or research progress |
| Rights and organizations | Actual property, office, voting, command, exit, audit, relief relationships | Existing rules and real power govern change; support is not automatically authority |
| Visible labels | A few descriptions, such as craft apprenticeships or maritime networks, with coverage evidence | Summaries of known facts, not perfect latent-state readings |

A group can join several compatible practices; only composite-profile population shares are exclusive. Religious definitions and formal affiliation are separate. No religion exists initially; founding fixes core doctrine. Religious, secular, and vocational schools can teach knowledge they actually possess; names confer no educational advantage.

## Eight attributes and their complete role

Each referee-private axis ranges from 0–10000 independently. Axes affect costly candidate actions and preferences, not wages, risks, law, health, education, or provision themselves. Low values are not ethnic essence; high values are not free benefits.

| Axis/code | Affected choices | Potential capability at high values | Constraints and possible low-value behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Openness O | Considering unfamiliar organizations, methods, cross-group hiring/cooperation | More knowledge, partners, and jobs accessible | Translation, verification, performance, integration cost; low O favors familiar networks without necessarily banning trade |
| Revisability R | Experiments, review, admitting failure, changing practice | Easier organization of funded comparisons/correction | Experiments and conversion sacrifice existing returns; low R preserves routines but may suppress contrary evidence |
| Exploration X | Surveys, travel, new occupations, unfamiliar environments | More known resources, routes, technical problems | Risk, training, lost local production; no guarantee of good discoveries |
| Agricultural stewardship A | Farming, restoration, storage, ecological provisioning roles | Participation in rewarded agriculture and ecological maintenance | Land/livelihood interests can impede reassignment; high A can support modern precision farming |
| Industrial workmanship I | Manufacture, maintenance, standards, engineering | Apprenticeships, upkeep, production organization | Materials, energy, occupational lock-in; no direct factory multiplier |
| Urban organization U | Housing, sanitation, facilities, settlement cooperation | Sustained services and complex settlements | Concentration needs maintenance/staff; rural people can support regional public works |
| Exchange E | Trade, bookkeeping, routes, regional specialization | Actual commercial and contracting participation | External delivery, price, credit, distribution risks; high E is not unconditional openness |
| Martial obligation M | Service, preparedness, command, armed organization | Recruitment and retention of supplied military work | Labor withdrawal, service risk, military interests; no direct individual combat bonus |

Do not add democracy, progress, morality, or interstellar maturity as universal cultural multipliers. Actual institutions/results define rights, services, concentration, and access; ability distributions define education. Societies with equal average axes can choose differently because organizations, assets, law, and loss-bearing differ.

## Reading the candidate graph

`Cxxx` values are internal template IDs. Seats obtain opaque handles only for known practices. Cognitive prerequisites use `&` for joint requirements and `|` for alternatives. They describe independent local formation of an actionable idea and form an acyclic graph.

Observation and teaching can introduce an advanced proposal without replaying its whole intellectual history. This waives neither adoption requirements nor hidden prerequisite visibility. Ideas arising and then failing are valid outcomes. Economic/technical/organizational columns concern adoption; recognition success cannot reveal unknown deficiencies for free. Visitors, distant sites, and cross-organizational contacts can come from within one civilization—different settlements, kin groups, and enterprises—so independent development does not require a rival's voluntary teaching.

Every practice records its proposal, applicable organizations/regions, known sources, participants, authorizations, resources/services, operating history, and reasons for suspension/exit. Cognitive parents normally need recognition, not continuing nationwide dominance. An industrial society may remember kin mutual aid without restoring all kinship rights.

## What determines branching?

The fixed graph offers recognizable methods; the world never randomly forces a corporate state or AI regime. Seeds change initial practices, organizations, sources, and audiences, affecting first contact with fixed practices. They cannot alter nodes, alternatives, discovery rules, or adoption thresholds. Nonadoption means conditions or choices differ, not that the node disappeared.

| Actual condition | Opportunities and resistance | Constraint on interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waterworks and seasonal coordination | C008 maintenance, allocation disputes, upstream/downstream organization | Rivers do not automatically cause centralization; contracted local bodies are possible |
| Ports and distant exchange | C012/C021/C024 reception, escrow, translation | Coasts do not automatically cause openness; blockade, losses, compensation, partners matter |
| Craft, tools, concentrated industry | C018/C030/C033/C034 skills, licenses, employment, representation | Steam/diesel does not automatically choose a political regime |
| Schools, printing, measurement | C023/C028/C029/C032 curricula, criticism, checking, mass instruction | Literacy broadens access, not guaranteed openness or government support |
| Concentrated property and networks | C035/C047/C048 coordination and essential-service dependence | Cyber society needs actual concentration, authority, lock-in, not merely chips |
| Distributed assets and maintenance | C034/C039/C052 joint governance and contributions | Common institutions face finance, free riding, delays; not automatically superior |
| Algorithms and government delegation | C049→C050→C053 increasing executable scope | Technical gates, authority, audit, provision, acceptable risk must actually hold |
| Orbital and distant work | C056/C057/C058/C060 long duties, delayed authority, support, archives | FTL need not be known; maritime, frontier, and long projects can supply experience |

Economic foundations constrain adoption scale and method. Early communities may understand joint deliberation without operating real-time national budgets, industrial corporations, or AI administration. Machines and revenue likewise do not automatically make owners surrender power. Technical/cultural feedback has monthly delays; neither unlocks the other in a same-month cycle.

## How practice feeds back into tendencies

These initial direction templates are not adoption rewards. Combine the observable outcome difference z with direction v, then apply 16's credibility, exposure, total evidence strength q, memory, and slow limits. `+`/`−` give direction; nonzero axes are normalized by total absolute magnitude: O+,E+ are +0.5 each; A+,I+,U+ are +1/3 each; R+ alone is +1. Omitted axes are zero; `—` is an all-zero vector. Only actual operation/results count; recognition alone changes no tendency. These are calibratable design starting points, not claims of inevitable historical effects.

This describes what participants may reinforce after successful execution, not a permanent association between institutional names and everybody's values. Failure can give negative z; unexposed groups do not update. Schools, officials, and media cannot count the same result three times. Researchers' successful openness need not change every farmer; successful AI administration does not itself confer political authorization.

Fixed implementation variants can coexist in the content version; disclose their operating rules to informed parties. Every variant remains in every match. Actual rights and participation determine use, not seed deletion or renaming for a favorable vector. Initial variants include:

| Variant | Actual practice and behavior | Direction replacement and economic effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exclusionary qualification | C018/C025 exclude qualified applicants by origin | Benefiting members use O−,I+; rejected groups record losses separately. Reduces accessible qualified labor rather than directly subtracting national science |
| Unchallengeable authority | C023/C027/C041 actually prohibit checking and punish it | Replace R+ with R−; if absent, add R−, then normalize. Suppression affects experiments/reporting, not physical failure |
| Testable boundaries | C014 religious schools or C027 colleges permit reproducible checking in a domain | R+ only for real verification experience; religious/secular names do not set direction |
| Identity-closed mutual aid | C010/C015 actually restrict aid to registered familiar members | Add O− to livelihood directions; outside reception needs C012 or changed rules; existing resident obligations remain |
| Privileged exemption | C009/C035/C041 powerful groups avoid maintenance, taxes, or service | Actual duty withdrawal reduces services; no national laziness axis. Beneficiaries and harmed groups record different experience |
| Status consumption and diverted maintenance | Any empowered organization moves upkeep resources to display | Actual materials/hours consumed; next-month equipment state changes; no inevitable decadence event |

Harmful practices may have no net benefit, or benefit a small group at others' expense. Stable operation does not mean everyone benefits; suppressed people, nonmembers, and cost bearers cannot be averaged away. Local experience and interpretation determine direction, not empire/ethnic names.

First replace/add variant directions, then normalize total absolute magnitude. Never apply both base and variant to the same practice/group/event. A zero vector still permits cultural transmission, rights changes, and organizational effects; it simply adds no extra directional impulse. An adopter cannot choose a favorable vector without changing actual rules.

## Authority conflicts and coexistence

Agricultural, industrial, urban, and commercial orientations can coexist. A state can contain guilds, firms, religious schools, and public schools, each using its actual assets, people, and rights. Resolve conflicts by object and authority, not a single national policy slot.

| Conflict | Possible coexistence | Actual decision required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household/public/corporate land | Different plots and terms | No incompatible control over the same use and time |
| Open research and secret licenses | Different results, versions, stages, uses | Published knowledge cannot be sold again as an exclusive secret; teaching, support, or new results may be sold |
| Corporate jurisdictions and public networks | Distinct facilities/scopes or legitimate joint operation | Define control, charging, emergency powers; both parties cannot count full capacity |
| Human reserves and highest AI administration | AI delegation outside reserved domains | Cannot claim both unreviewable decisions and independent correction for the same action; define emergencies/exceptions in advance |
| Local autonomy and global highest duties | Autonomy within delegated budgets/security/adjudication | A federation label cannot imply surrendered highest powers; G96 checks actual execution |

Reform faces existing authorizations and opponents. Compensation, narrower scope, staging, replacement services, and real coercion are possible. A cultural payment cannot convert everyone to support. Institutions may change visible conduct first while norms remain slow. Reversal does not erase the experience.

## AI rule and cyber society

Cyber society combines computing networks, augmentation or automation, with property, access, labor, and governance arrangements. C048 can concentrate supply/investment while tying housing, employment, identity, and network access together. C052 joint operation, C051 reserved human powers, ordinary public services, and conventional firms can compete or mix. There is no automatic nationwide cyberpunk skin when an era unlocks.

The AI political path has three steps: C049 validated advice; C050 limited accountable delegation; C053 highest administrative execution. Each needs valid data, in-world hardware, inference/maintenance, actual authority, and failure handling. Passing a science, finance, or policing model test does not establish capability in every other domain.

In-world AI is not assumed conscious. Its authority comes from institutions or actual political takeover. Versioned bounded governance rules constrain behavior to known information and authority. It cannot read private referee truth or call an extra external model to think free for a contestant. The player remains the same civilization's decision source; orders must pass actual authority chains. If highest power moves to another independent political entity, [15](15-victory-and-ftl.md) re-evaluates control and governance continuity; calling it a software replacement does not bypass regime change.

AI administration can use low-latency scheduling and continuous data, but centralization, correlated model error, power failure, falsified sensors, and noncompliant executors can undermine it. Human administration also faces information/execution problems. Neither automatically wins or fails. Authority rests on verifiable tasks and resources, not an AI-level national bonus.

C059 is a recognition plan only. Any actual machine institution needs independent identity, assets, operating costs, liability, and legal scope. Copying programs creates no free people, demand, research teams, independent voters, or player seats. One controller cannot multiply charter votes by copying agents. Digital population requires a separate complete production/identity/exit/rights model; this version does not implement it implicitly. Preparing for unfamiliar subjects does not mean aliens already exist or players know they will encounter them.

## Cultural capabilities for an interstellar society

There is no single interstellar personality. Complex systems must operate across distance, political terms, and groups. The following are auditable organizational tasks, not an interstellar culture score.

| Problem | Composable implementations | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long projects and government succession | C056 inherited duties; public, corporate, family, or common long-term assets | Actual finance, maintenance, handover, debt, revision |
| Communication delay and local crises | C057 bounded local powers, human or AI executors | Command arrival, lost-contact action, boundaries, later review |
| Population and life support | C055/C058 and existing rural/urban/industrial provision | Food, water, energy, maintenance, evacuation, resident coverage; no missing people in transit |
| High-energy engineering and failures | C045 independent checks, multilevel review or hierarchical inspection | Actual trials, shutdown, correction, replacement, incident responsibility |
| Knowledge and rights continuity | C060 distributed records, recovery drills, skill transmission | Recoverable records and people able to use them, not file counts alone |
| Heterogeneous membership and distribution | C040/C042/C043 or equivalent executable governance | Qualifications, budgets, security, adjudication, conflict handling; no required cultural homogenization |

Local traditions need not disappear. Agricultural stewardship can extend to life support; industrial cultures can emphasize reliable manufacture and engineering accountability; trade can support contracts and shared insurance; military organizations can develop sustained provisioning and bounded command. Each still needs missing capabilities.

C061/C062/C063 are three reference arrangements for global duties, not new cultural victory locks. Other lawful arrangements satisfying highest authority, sustained services, and FTL acceptance can win. Integration does not require one religion, culture, or popular leader. C064 organizes distant tasks; it must not reveal FTL text or hidden endpoint labels before the objective is known. In v1.0, these arrangements can deliver one contestant's integration, never a separate cooperative victory mode.

## Counterexamples and acceptance

- Equal writing, printing, and money do not yield equal education if one society lacks teachers, student time, and practice. Selecting C032 cannot fill the gap. Early craftspeople/scholars must not count as knowing nothing merely because they lack modern credentials.
- Equal port wealth can produce different openness demands when merchants bear losses themselves versus transferring them to farmers. National trade value cannot update everyone's O.
- C029 success updates only actual participants and recipients of credible results. Reposting an experiment or splitting newspapers/schools does not increase q.
- The same AI, data, and power execute only their actually authorized scopes. Highest authority does not improve model accuracy or increase contestants' tokens.
