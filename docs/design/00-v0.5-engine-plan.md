# Before the Stars v0.5 — underlying engine plan

Historical engine baseline, translated into English. The [v1.0 plan](30-complete-plan-v1.0.md) now governs scope, competition and the shared clock; [31](31-knowledge-and-culture-v1.0.md) governs knowledge/culture corrections and [32](32-population-and-education-v1.0.md) simplifies population. The [dynamic-development specification](20-dynamic-development-v0.6.md), [industrial overview](23-industrial-development-v0.7.md) and [postindustrial overview](28-postindustrial-plan-v0.8.md) subsequently expanded content. Existing ledger, war, contract and sustained-victory principles remain where not superseded.

This stage wrote design, not game code. Building on v0.4, it unified state, time, resource competition, knowledge and culture, and compared budget policies from identical starts on paper. Internal variables, algorithms and causal tables are designer material and must not become a formal seat knowledge package.

The result was a rule structure and reproducible local arithmetic, not a complete content pack, running game, model competition or measured balance. Distinguish specified transitions, parameters awaiting freeze, and strategic claims awaiting validation.

## Few entities, meaningful complexity

Do not simulate every person's psychology or every screw. Entities are regions/connections, settlements/land, population groups, organizations/governments, facilities/accounts, knowledge/practices, projects/contracts, formations/events. Historical household-stage entities are superseded by the v1.0 aggregate representation.

Complexity comes from competing for the same hours, materials, cash, equipment time, services and rights. National summaries are not a second resource pool: wealth is not treasury cash; total population is not qualified manpower; past technology adoption is not present machinery; a cultural label is not actual organizational participation.

Keep templates finite, population grouped with necessary distributions, and causal work local to real dependencies. Do not turn every resident or cultural axis into an agent. Private organizations use bounded rules rather than an LLM deciding physical outcomes. Compress equivalent computation without deleting minorities, shipments or debts.

## Time

| Clock | Unit/use | Prohibition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World time | `SimMonth` for people, maintenance, production, contracts, diffusion, war and acceptance | No births per click or research per tool call |
| Decision windows | A shared calendar fixed before opening | No secret-disaster pause or calendar change caused by one civilization's technology |
| Wall time | Referee computation, model milliseconds, tokens and costs | External model compute cannot become industrial compute inside the game |

The historical short fixture used approximately 80 twelve-month windows, or 960 months, for a compressed capability chain. It did not claim human history occurred in 80 years. v1.0 now fixes a candidate standard of 240 such rounds/H2880; longer literal historical calendars are outside this baseline. Actual ages, education, lifespan and maintenance do not receive era multipliers. The earlier two-negotiation-window description is superseded by contact selection and five windows in [29](29-diplomacy-communication-v0.8.1.md).

Standing production, procurement/sales, maintenance, relief, project and military rules operate across the window. Reallocate after actual monthly receipts. Government cannot be restricted to annual purchases while private actors adjust monthly for free. Automation reads only received information through known bounded contingency templates. A shorter-window scenario, if introduced later, must declare its shared calendar in advance rather than insert private action opportunities.

Finish ledgers/events before projecting observations. Sealed orders cannot react to prices or enemy commands that have not yet occurred. New goods become available no earlier than next month; current power, cooling, network and compute settle through the service dependency order. A newly accepted recipe becomes usable no earlier than next month. See technical/07 for full timing.

## System dependencies

| System | Reads | Changes | Competition/delay | Observable phenomena |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geography/land/resources | Water, climate, deposits, rights, known methods, maintenance | Extractable amounts, fertility, access, environmental burden | Land use, recovery, transport and extraction | Surveyed quality, yield, travel and local damage |
| Agriculture/mining/industry | Qualified hours, equipment, opening stock, services, recipes | New goods, losses, equipment condition, emissions | Scarcest supported task quantity; goods available next month | Orders, downtime, unit inputs and delivery |
| Cities/villages/services | Residents, land, commuting, housing, water, sanitation, routes | Reachable services, effective hours, health burden | Persistent building and maintenance inputs | Local housing, supply, travel and coverage |
| Fiscal/trade | Accounts, real title, known prices, orders, contracts, transport | Payments, reservations, ownership, shipments, debt | Future income is not cash; transit is not spot stock | Inventory, cash flow, quotes, performance and risk |
| Population | Age, supply, health, care, housing, jobs, norms, safety | Births, deaths, migration, labor supply | Growth and service burdens; registry is not truth | Covered flows, age structure and burdens |
| Education/skills | Courses, teachers/students, materials, practice posts, equipment | Task qualifications and distributions | Training time, teacher supply and useful employment | Enrollment, completion, practice, jobs and vacancies |
| Research/projects | Known problems, evidence, teams, samples, instruments, energy, compute, lawful cooperation | Knowledge, validation, recipes, facilities, leads | Discovery, understanding, prototype and adoption need actual tasks | Measurements, shortages, known evidence and results |
| Culture/religion | Local experience, transmission, contact, practice, fixed doctrines, institutions | Slow norms, participation, composition and commitments | Persistence and generations; followers are not free faith currency | Behavior, surveys and attributed descriptions |
| Factions/politics | Assets, members, offices, rules, interests and expectations | Consent, organization, supply/refusal and execution | People/rights cannot supply hours or votes twice | Demands, terms, performance and imperfect support estimates |
| War/security | Volunteers/recruits, authority, skills, equipment, command, supply, terrain | Position, control, losses, damage, governance duties | Training/logistics draw on the economy; occupation is not instant capacity | Known formations, supply, reports and actual administration |
| Global integration/FTL | Valid government, production, training, deployment, repair, replacements and trials | Continuous windows and victory time | G96, F24 and two valid trials at least 12 months apart | Revealed acceptance evidence and known deficiencies |

Apply a causal effect once. Education creates qualified hours; research consumes them; production uses new methods. Do not add separate education/research/GDP/cultural-prosperity multipliers for that same effect. Upstream losses propagate through stock, wages, supply and maintenance rather than a national-collapse multiplier.

## Dynamic development is more than a hidden progress bar

Complete graphs and discovery rules remain fixed within the content version. Worlds and actual experience produce different known subgraphs and implementation conditions. Paths have material, energy, measurement, control, manufacturing and organizational interfaces. Punk traditions describe accumulated capabilities, not mutually exclusive civilization skins. Large-scale adoption must be affordable. Modern institutions cannot appear from a button without commercial organization, accounting, enforceable rights and division of labor.

Knowing a practice and adopting it differ. Profiles change slowly through population and local experience. Agriculture, industry, urban life, exploration and trade coexist. Openness is not always good; conservatism is not low intelligence. Practices act through correction of knowledge, cooperation, occupations, organizational cost and risk. Seats build their own evidence-based models without receiving secret axes or weights.

Religion starts absent and emerges locally. Founding doctrines stay fixed, while members, assets, influence and interpretations change. Culture/religion/education affect recruitment, role suitability, learning and cooperation, not three additional national combat multipliers. Actual personnel transitions follow war rules.

Population does not use a fixed growth function. Subsistence, sanitation, expectations and education/care/housing costs affect demographic flows. Better mortality does not guarantee more births. Food access, storage, extraction, disease burden, government and migration mediate crises. Regions may plateau, decline and recover without predetermined real-civilization destinies. v1.0 preserves these relationships with simpler fertility/health aggregates.

## What the strategy comparison establishes

The [matched paper comparison](../reviews/05-budget-strategy-comparison.md) fixes assets, knowledge, people, prices and external conditions for education-first, research-first, military-first and visible-bottleneck policies. It compares spending, retained cash, capacity, skills, tasks, maintenance and endgame preparation rather than converting budget ceilings directly to output.

Two levels are distinct: calculable development accounts, and acceptance schedules under common endgame conditions. The latter needs both integration and military FTL; research leadership is only one part. Without complete content, do not invent a precise inevitable victory year from the primitive start.

Challenge both endless expansion of an already oversupplied input and myopic repair of the currently shortest input. Bottleneck adaptation is a broad strategy family with conflicting choices, including temporarily high education or military spending, not a universally optimal action list.

The real questions are whether a measured shortage is correct; what the next shortage will be; whether to build, import, license or jointly research; whom to compensate; whether early capacity will sit idle; whether redundancy protects supply; and whether knowledge can become production and global authority. Prices, security, intentions, lead time and conversion costs alter the answer.

Reward remains the [full-victory rule](15-victory-and-ftl.md). Failure cannot improve its score through delay or early surrender. Feedback is data, not extra points. Culture, education, GDP, military power and discoveries do not independently award reward.

## Cross-system repairs in this stage

| Problem | Specified repair | Required counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delaying all output prevents same-month power/compute | Goods cross a month boundary; nonstorable services follow dependency order | Fuel/startup power cannot be created by a circular service chain |
| Markets clear before workers have first-month food money | Real opening reserves and explicit funded wage advances; net month-end settlement | No full wage paid again after an advance; future wages create no cash |
| New advances consume already-due funds | Protect effective due obligations first; advances follow priority and count as spending | One monthly limit cannot finance advance and full final payment |
| Future shipments enter spot prices/consumption | Spot uses arrived accepted stock; future goods use dated contracts | Intercepted goods cannot already have been eaten at destination |
| Government yearly orders versus private monthly operation | Same bounded standing purchase/sale/production rules and information | Actor type does not create free action frequency |
| Research multipliers exceed samples/instruments | Minimum actual supported task quantity; evidence quality separate | More experts cannot overcome zero instruments or samples |
| Budget shares are destroyed to force balance to win | Separate ceiling, commitment, procurement, payment and output; release unused ceiling | Changing only an unused ceiling cannot change world outcomes |
| Always fix the current bottleneck becomes mechanical | Distinguish observed shortages, long complements, error, risk and rivals | Identical current shortages with different future threats can favor different actions |
| Repeated propaganda/group splitting accelerates culture | Deduplicate real events/recipients; inherit anchors/remainders and first-contact age | Replaying events or changing IDs does not refresh learning/shock allowance |
| Tiny evidence gets normalized into a full update | Retain evidence mass and shrink target movement by q | 1% evidence cannot produce a full change; splitting does not increase q |
| Environment lacks one effective-entry phase | Commit prior changes/weather at opening; debit resources once; externalities next month | Upstream/downstream cannot both withdraw the same water or double-charge a disaster |
| Research completion wins early or disarmament shrinks tests | Fixed loads, G96, F24 and trial evidence audited together | Prototype-only, borrowed vessel and population deletion do not qualify |

## Content that must be frozen before implementation

Sophisticated rules cannot mean unspecified rules. Every referenced item needs a complete entry in one versioned pack, even with a small initial set. Unknown fields cannot default to zero cost or success.

| Table | Required content | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantities/accounts | Units, bounds, precision, rounding/remainder ownership, source/sink events | Compatible arithmetic and no split/merge gains |
| Scenario/start/calendar | Months, ages/assets, startup food/working capital, environment/communication | Sustainable primitive start; calendar leaks no secret |
| Geography/connections/hazards | Quality, recovery, water/ecology, travel, correlated risks | Replayable versioned generation, coherent geography |
| Recipes/facilities/services | Per-task inputs/outputs, skills, maintenance, dependencies/startup | Every input has a source; service graph settles; loops need real startup stock |
| Organizations/employment | Visible options, price/reserve rules, risks, ranking/switching | Duplicate options ineffective; actors not omniscient; hours/payments unique |
| Population/health | Age hazards, supply/care/migration response, norms and bounded information | Traceable stable, low-fertility and crisis/recovery fixtures; v1.0 replaces detailed family stages |
| Education/jobs | Prerequisites, teacher/student hours, equipment, qualification maintenance and switching | Primitive teaching works; a qualification is not a free job |
| Technology/engineering | Discovery entries, dependencies, tasks, validation, transmission, adoption/maintenance | Structural and affordable reference routes without infinite outside markets |
| Culture/practices/religion | Private behavior mappings, evidence normalization, direction vectors, memory, generations, doctrine limits | Slow local differences, no double counting, safe projection |
| Politics/contracts | Powers, roles, consent, deadlines, fees and final disposal | Partial failure, isolation and bankruptcy preserve assets/claims |
| Military/endgame | Three roles per layer, equipment/training/tactics/logistics, fixed FTL loads | Strength from actual capability; conquest/federation face equivalent acceptance |
| Observation/protocol/evaluation | Templates, metric units/coverage, action/message limits, reward isolation | Full human/agent operation with no hidden requirements or objectives leaked |

Awaiting calibration does not mean omitting a function. Structure, conservation, phases and transitions are specified; parameters need an initial version before a scenario is fully calculable. Revise later using preregistered seed comparisons, counterexamples and held-out results, never retroactively alter the current match around its winner.

## Next design acceptance

1. Freeze a minimal complete content set with feasible subsistence, urban industry, route divergence, diplomacy/war and endgame.
2. Repeat accounts in that version with nonomniscient partners, interruptions, threats and varied endowments. Declare external changes before comparing outcomes.
3. Generate human/agent panels from the same authorized accounts; show inputs, unmet demand, downtime, waiting and uncertainty rather than prose alone or one national-power score.
4. Check a legal action chain from start to victory, especially primitive organization, standing operations, contract response and final acceptance.
5. Only then begin P0 contracts and engine work. Determinism, multicore equivalence, conservation, authority and noninterference remain implementation gates, not passed tests in this document.
