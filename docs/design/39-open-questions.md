# 39. Deeper trees, boost and stations: decisions to confirm

Companion to `39-deeper-trees-boost-stations.md`. Only real choices are listed, each with
the design's decision, the alternative and a recommendation. Items 1-7 should be settled
before slice 6b/6c, items 8-14 before slice 14.

1. **Size of the graphs.** Designed: 300 technologies (157 + 143) and 174 practices
   (67 + 107). Alternative: a smaller expansion (about 250 and 150) that is cheaper to
   write lore for and to calibrate. **Recommendation: as designed**, merged in tranches by
   era (antiquity first). Every new node needs two encyclopedia passages before merge, and
   the cut list drops the sealed nodes and tradition institutions first.

2. **Tradition branches hidden in practice.** Designed: a branch node's evidence comes only
   from its tradition's works, plus contact with a holder, capture or a paid study. This
   revises doc 37 §1.2 ("traditions never hide a technology"); nothing is *locked*.
   Alternative: keep branch nodes open to everyone's ordinary evidence (no hidden punk
   knowledge). **Recommendation: as designed.** It is what makes the punk eras feel
   different and keeps a seat from collecting every branch.

3. **Strength of path dependence.** Designed: up to evidence ×0.40, work ×1.50 and a
   discovery cap while one standard holds ≥ 75% of the installed base; refutation halves the
   lock; a revisit study lifts it; the two critical members (T053, T111) are capped at ×0.80
   and ×1.10 with no cap on discovery. Alternative: a mild version (×0.70 and ×1.20, no
   discovery cap). **Recommendation: as designed**, with the mild version as the first
   calibration fallback if families make seats stagnate.

4. **Hard-exclusive practices.** Designed: 17 groups whose members cannot be held at the
   same time (repeal first), including the three world orders C061-C063, C041 vs C042 and
   C051 vs C053; each side leaves a route to C064. Alternative: tensions only (both held,
   with a stability cost). **Recommendation: hard exclusives.** The tree only becomes a
   choice when one road closes another.

5. **Institutional load.** Designed: each established practice adds load by era; capacity
   comes from administrative offices and five capacity practices; overload costs stability
   and reform speed. Alternative: no load, only era costs and reform slots.
   **Recommendation: include**; it is the main brake on "adopt everything", and it is seventh
   on the cut list.

6. **Native AI and public content.** Designed: the native AI and the `Expert` test policy may
   use compiled static content (the victory-critical set, families, branches, the building
   tree), scoring only cards present in their observation. Alternative: the AI derives
   everything from observation alone (no critical-set table). **Recommendation: as
   designed.** The content is public in the repository; using it leaks no match state, and
   without it the Hard AI is unlikely to reach doc 36's win targets on a twice larger graph.

7. **Practice costs of the victory-critical set.** Designed: the 35 critical practices keep
   their present costs while all others move to the steeper era costs. Alternative: one
   era cost table for every practice (simpler, but institutions could become the long pole
   of victory). **Recommendation: protect the critical practices.**

8. **Boost does not decay.** Designed: a cap set by launch stages and integration halls; at
   the cap launch sites stand down and draw no inputs. Alternative: a monthly decay of the
   stock (1%), with no stand-down. **Recommendation: no decay**: it conserves goods, needs no
   waste ledger and still makes a big push a planning problem.

9. **What boost covers.** Designed: only the climb from the Earth's surface to low Earth
   orbit; everything beyond stays doc 37's propellant, berths, ratios and windows; the
   doc-35 orbital station and yard keep their national-pool inputs as a "ground segment"
   and pay boost only for construction mass and the aboard crew's consumables.
   Alternative: all station and yard inputs launched with boost (the yard's 10 kt of
   components a month would need a dozen launch complexes). **Recommendation: as
   designed.**

10. **Check 13.** Designed: "a launch stage at the launch-complex stage or later that made
    boost (or stood down at its cap) in each of the last 12 months, and an Earth-orbit
    station with a shipyard module". Alternative: keep doc 35's wording (launch complex
    operating, orbital yard as an Earth facility). **Recommendation: as designed**; it adds
    about 8,500 t of boost once and at most a year to the critical path.

11. **Stations as objects, not regions.** Designed: crews only (no births), counted as
    "aboard stations" in the seat's population; a rotating ring attaches a station to the
    seat's site in that zone for residents. Alternative: every station is a region with
    population blocks. **Recommendation: objects** (cheaper, and it keeps doc 37's 19 sites
    as the places where people live).

12. **Launch failures and crew deaths.** Designed: an addressed draw per month of launches;
    losses of payload, crews and a month of production; debris. Alternative: a fixed 1%
    deterministic loss of spent boost. **Recommendation: include failures**; the fixed loss
    is the fallback (cut item 5).

13. **Space elevator and skyhook.** Designed: AI-era megaproject (one per seat, equatorial,
    up to 24 kt of boost a month) and a LEO skyhook module. Alternative: chemical, reusable
    and tradition launch stages only. **Recommendation: include**, second on the cut list.

14. **Merge timing of doc 37's 19 technologies.** Designed: move them from slice 14 to slice
    6b with the other new nodes (their works stay in 14), so that the graph and its pacing
    are calibrated once. Alternative: keep them in 14 and merge the chapter-39 nodes that
    depend on them (17 nodes) then. **Recommendation: move them to 6b.**

15. **Reachability targets.** Designed: an expert planner wins in ≥ 80% of 32 seeds with a
    median AD 2100-2200 and a 10th percentile no earlier than 2050; the Hard native AI keeps
    doc 36's targets; if the expert is late, loosen critical-path thresholds first, never
    breadth. Alternative: tie the expert to doc 36's Hard targets only. **Recommendation: as
    designed**, plus four qualitative LLM-agent matches through MCP in slice 17.
